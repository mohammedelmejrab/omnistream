Samsung Electronics and the International Olympic Committee (IOC) are spotlighting young innovators from Samsung Solve for Tomorrow (SFT) programme at the Youth Olympic Games Dakar 2026, the first Olympic Games to be held in Africa. The innovators are sharing solutions they have developed to address social challenges in their communities. Launched in 2010, Solve for Tomorrow is Samsung’s global corporate social responsibility (CSR) program for young people, helping them use science, technology, engineering, and mathematics (STEM) to address challenges in their communities. Through education and mentoring, students learn to identify problems, develop ideas, and build and refine prototypes. The global ambassador initiative gives them opportunities to present their work internationally. The activities in Dakar build on Samsung and the IOC’s collaboration on SFT’s Sport & Tech theme and their joint delivery of the Samsung Solve for Tomorrow Impact Programme. The ambassadors began by tackling problems in their schools and neighborhoods; now they are sharing their solutions with the world. Solve for Tomorrow Exhibition at Dakar 2026 At the SFT exhibition, organized in cooperation with the IOC at the Corniche West Live Site, visitors could explore the SFT ambassadors’ solutions and hear about the challenges they faced and how they refined their ideas. By discussing their work and exchanging feedback with people from different backgrounds, these young people can consider how their technology might serve a wider range of needs. The exhibition is part of a wider conversation and shared learning experience at Dakar 2026. The exhibition is scheduled to run through November 13, 2026. During the exhibition opening, through the panel discussion “Youth at the Heart of Global Transformation”, Samsung shared with the Senegalese students the SFT programme’s objectives to encourage young people to solve problems through technology and sport, highlighting how young people can use sport and the Olympic spirit in taking on challenges and working together in everyday life. As part of the programme the ambassadors also met with IOC Young Leaders from Kenya and Lebanon for a knowledge exchange workshop with focus on social challenges of young people in Africa. SFT students who have used technology to tackle local problems will exchange experiences with IOC Young Leaders who have used sport to bring about change in their communities. After the workshop, the students have also visited the “Olympic Social Impact” booth, presented by IOC Young Leaders, in the Youth Olympic Village to learn about athlete experience engagement. From Paris to LA, Supporting the Next Steps for Young Innovators Samsung and the IOC first introduced SFT ambassadors at Paris 2024 and affirmed their shared commitment to developing future talent at Milano Cortina 2026 and beyond. In August 2026, they jointly launched the Solve for Tomorrow Impact Programme to support young innovators in developing their ideas and putting them to use in their communities and this programme will run until end of January 2028. As the Samsung’s Olympic partnership continues toward the LA28 Games, Samsung and the IOC plan to sustain their collaboration on SFT’s Sport & Tech theme and the SFT Impact Program.
