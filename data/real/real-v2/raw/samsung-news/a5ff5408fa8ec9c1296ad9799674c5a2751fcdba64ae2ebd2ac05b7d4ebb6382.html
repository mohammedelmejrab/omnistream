Choosing the right TV is a long-term decision that involves several factors — including screen size, picture quality, brightness, viewing environment, and preferred content. 

Advances in TV technology have expanded the options available, from OLED, QLED, and Neo QLED to the latest Micro RGB. AI capabilities now go beyond picture and sound optimization to support content discovery, device connectivity, and personalized viewing experiences. As a result, consumers are asking more specific questions about screen size, AI features, and their viewing needs rather than simply “Which TV is the best?” 

Samsung Newsroom outlines key criteria and answers common questions about buying a TV.

How To Choose the Right TV Size 

When choosing a TV size, viewing distance and available space are key considerations. For comfortable, immersive viewing, the recommended distance is approximately 1.2 times the screen size in centimeters, based on a 40-degree field of view.1

For typical living room seating arrangements, recommended screen sizes vary by viewing distance: 

  • 1.5 to 2.0 meters: 43- to 55-inch TVs 
  • 2.0 to 2.5 meters: 55- to 65-inch TVs 
  • 2.3 to 3.0 meters: 65- to 75-inch TVs 
  • 3.0 meters or more: 85-inch or larger TVs 

These are general guidelines, as comfortable viewing distances may vary depending on viewing habits — such as watching content with subtitles or as a family. 

Larger screens enhance immersion but may cause eye strain if too large for the space. Samsung offers TVs ranging from 32 to 114 inches to accommodate different spaces and viewing preferences. 

How Lighting Affects TV Selection 

Viewing times and lighting conditions vary from home to home, making the viewing environment an important consideration when choosing a TV. In bright spaces, screen brightness and reflection reduction are key, while contrast ratio matters more in darker settings. 

For sunlit living rooms, Neo QLED TVs provide high brightness for clear viewing. OLED TVs, meanwhile, deliver exceptional contrast — making them well suited to darker spaces. 

However, advances in display technology are making lighting conditions less of a constraint. OLED TVs with Glare Free technology minimize reflections, maintaining a clear picture even in brightly lit rooms.2  

AI Gamma technology analyzes scene brightness in real time and automatically adjusts gamma levels based on ambient lighting. It brightens dark scenes to preserve detail in well-lit rooms and reduces glare while enhancing contrast in darker settings. These adjustments help minimize eye strain without compromising picture clarity. 

How Samsung TV Display Technologies Compare 

Display technologies produce light and color differently, each offering advantages depending on viewing conditions and intended use: 

  • OLED: Relies on self-emissive pixels rather than a backlight to deliver near-perfect blacks and fast response times. 
  • Mini LED: Employs smaller LEDs than conventional backlights to produce strong contrast, particularly for HDR content. 
  • Neo QLED: Combines Quantum Matrix Technology for precise Quantum Mini LED backlight control with Quantum Dot technology that accurately reproduces 2,140 Pantone colors and 110 skin tones. 
  • Micro RGB: Features individually controlled, microscopic red, green, and blue (RGB) backlights for a wide color gamut and precise color reproduction. 

OLED TVs excel at reproducing deep blacks, making them ideal for movies and TV shows in dark rooms. In brighter spaces, TVs with advanced backlight control have an advantage. Mini LED TVs provide strong contrast at an affordable price, while premium Neo QLED TVs combine rich colors from Samsung’s proprietary quantum dot film with powerful AI upscaling. For vivid, accurate colors and lifelike picture quality faithful to the original content, Micro RGB TVs are another option. 

How Content Shapes TV Choices 

Different types of content call for different TV features: 

  • Movies: High Dynamic Range (HDR) brings out subtle details and colors in both bright and dark scenes, creating greater depth and realism. 
  • Live Sports: AI-powered features enhance the viewing experience through real-time picture and sound adjustments. AI Soccer Mode Pro, AI Motion Enhancer, and AI Sound Controller Pro work together to deliver clearer action and more immersive stadium sound. 
  • Video Games: High refresh rates ensure smooth motion, while low input lag enables faster responses to player commands. AI Gaming Optimizer automatically adjusts picture and sound settings based on the game genre. 
  • Art: Samsung’s Art TV lineup, including The Frame and The Frame Pro, displays renowned artworks when not in use — transforming living spaces into galleries. Customizable bezels, the One Connect Box, and Wireless One Connect create a picture frame-like appearance, while Samsung Art Store provides access to more than 5,000 artworks through a subscription. 

How AI Makes TVs Smarter 

Unlike conventional TVs, Samsung AI TVs automatically optimize picture and sound while supporting content discovery and information searches. 

  • AI Upscaling Pro: Enhances older or lower-resolution content for sharper visuals on high-resolution screens and more immersive sound. 
  • AI Soccer Mode Pro: Adjusts picture and sound to recreate the atmosphere of live soccer matches. 
  • AI Gaming Optimizer: Recognizes game genres and tailors picture and sound settings accordingly. 
  • Vision AI Companion: Connects with AI services to help viewers search for information and discover related content on-screen. 

When choosing a TV, consumers should look for AI features that match their viewing habits — from automatic picture and sound optimization for sports, movies, and games to on-screen information searches. 

TV Buying Guide at a Glance

Q. What TV size is ideal for a living room? 

For viewing distances of 2.3 meters or more, a 75-inch or larger TV is recommended. With 4K resolution now widespread, larger screens maintain sharp picture quality even at closer distances. However, sufficient installation space and viewing distance remain important.

Q. What TV size is ideal for a bedroom? 

Bedrooms typically have shorter viewing distances and limited space, making 42- to 50-inch TVs generally suitable. The ideal size depends on the distance from the bed to the screen, as well as the TV’s overall dimensions including the stand and bezel.

Q. Should consumers choose a 65- or 75-inch TV? 

The recommended viewing distance is approximately 2.0 meters for a 65-inch TV and 2.3 meters for a 75-inch model. If possible, compare both sizes from the intended viewing distances before deciding. 

Q. Can lower-resolution content still look sharp on a 4K TV? 

Yes. AI Upscaling enhances lower-resolution content to match 4K displays for sharper viewing. Results may vary by model and source material. 

Q. Do OLED TVs reduce glare? 

OLED TVs combine exceptional contrast with Glare Free technology to minimize reflections. The 2025 Samsung S95F OLED TV features a low-reflection layer that diffuses external light while preserving contrast and color, even in bright rooms. 

Q. Should consumers choose QLED or Neo QLED TVs? 

Neo QLED TVs provide high brightness and precise contrast, while QLED TVs deliver vivid colors at a more affordable price. Both use Quantum Dot technology, but Neo QLED TVs feature smaller Mini LED backlights for more precise backlight control. 

Q. What is the difference between HDR and non-HDR TVs? 

HDR TVs reproduce a wider range of brightness and preserve details in highlights and shadows, creating greater depth and realism. 

Q. Can I use a Samsung Smart TV without a subscription? 

Yes. Samsung Smart TVs offer features and free content without a paid subscription. Models supporting Samsung TV Plus provide free channels featuring news, entertainment, dramas, and movies.3 

Samsung Art Store allows users to select artworks and the monthly curated Art Store Streams collection for free. A subscription unlocks access to the full collection of more than 5,000 artworks.4 

Q. Which is better for gaming, OLED or Neo QLED TVs? 

OLED TVs deliver fast response times and near-perfect blacks, while Neo QLED TVs provide high brightness and strong HDR performance. Both offer high refresh rates, low input lag, and Game Modes, making room lighting and preferred game genres important considerations. 

Q. How can theater-quality sound be achieved with a Samsung TV? 

Q-Symphony enables compatible Samsung TVs and sound devices to play audio together, delivering optimized surround sound based on room layout and device placement.
