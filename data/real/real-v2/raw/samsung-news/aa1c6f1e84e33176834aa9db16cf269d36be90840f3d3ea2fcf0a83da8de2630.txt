Samsung is expanding language support for Galaxy AI and Bixby, bringing these experiences to more users in their own language. Galaxy AI is expanding language support across more of its features, starting with those that translate, transcribe, and summarize. Call Assist, Note Assist, Interpreter, and Transcript Assist now include Hungarian and Malay, bringing features such as Live Translate to 24 languages. 1 ▲ Live Translate of a phone call in Malay-English (Image simulated for illustrative purposes. Actual UX/UI may differ.) Elsewhere in Galaxy AI, Now Nudge, which suggests a relevant next step based on what is on screen, adds eight languages 2 to reach 24 as well. Bixby is a device agent that offers intuitive device control through natural conversation. Users can ask for what they need in their own words, without having to know the exact name of a setting or where to find it in a menu. With the addition of Polish, Thai, and Vietnamese, Bixby now supports 12 languages, 3 allowing more Galaxy users to control their devices more effortlessly. Bixby’s new languages will begin rolling out on October 14, 4 with the new Galaxy AI languages to follow in the coming weeks. 5 Galaxy AI supports 24 languages: Arabic, Chinese (China mainland, Hong Kong, Taiwan), Dutch, English (Australia, India, UK, U.S.), Filipino, French (Canada, France), German, Gujarati, Hindi, Hungarian, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Malay, Polish, Portuguese (Brazil, Europe), Romanian, Russian, Spanish (Mexico, Spain, U.S.), Swedish, Thai, Turkish, and Vietnamese.
