Marvell Appoints Three New Independent Directors to its Board SANTA CLARA, Calif. – November 12, 2026 - Marvell Technology, Inc. (NASDAQ: MRVL), a leader in data infrastructure semiconductor solutions, today announced the appointment of Donna C. Morris, Douglas R. Bettinger, and Sasan K. Goodarzi as independent directors, effective November 12, 2026. The appointments expand Marvell’s Board from seven to ten directors, adding extensive executive leadership experience and deep expertise in technology, finance, talent strategy, and scaling global businesses. Mr. Bettinger has also been appointed to Marvell’s Audit Committee and Ms. Morris and Mr. Goodarzi have been appointed to Marvell’s Executive Compensation Committee. “Marvell is entering an extraordinary period of growth as demand for AI infrastructure continues to accelerate,” said Matt Murphy, Chairman and CEO of Marvell. “Donna, Doug, and Sasan are exceptional leaders who have helped shape and guide some of the world’s most successful companies. Their collective experience, strategic insight, and proven ability to lead at scale will bring tremendous value to our Board as we execute our strategy and capitalize on the opportunities ahead. I’m thrilled to welcome all three to Marvell and look forward to working with them.” Ms. Morris serves as Executive Vice President and Chief People Officer of Walmart Inc., where she leads the company’s global people strategy and serves on its executive committee. Prior to joining Walmart, she spent nearly 18 years at Adobe Inc., holding senior leadership positions including Chief Human Resources Officer and Executive Vice President, Employee Experience. Ms. Morris previously served on Marvell’s Board from 2018 to 2020. Her deep expertise in talent strategy, leadership development, and organizational culture will be valuable as Marvell continues to grow revenue and expand its workforce while preserving its unique culture. Mr. Bettinger serves as Executive Vice President and Chief Financial Officer of Lam Research Corporation. Prior to joining Lam Research in 2013, he served as Senior Vice President and Chief Financial Officer of Avago Technologies (now Broadcom Inc.), Vice President of Finance and Corporate Controller at Xilinx, and Chief Financial Officer of 24/7 Customer. Earlier in his career, he held several senior finance positions at Intel Corporation. With extensive semiconductor industry experience as a CFO and senior executive, Mr. Bettinger brings a proven track record of financial leadership and operational excellence from helping guide some of the industry's leading technology companies through growth and innovation. He currently serves on the board of directors of Lattice Semiconductor Corporation, where he is a member of the Audit Committee. Mr. Goodarzi serves as Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of Intuit Inc. Since becoming CEO in 2019, he has led Intuit’s transformation into a financial intelligence platform powering prosperity for its customers while more than tripling its revenue. Prior to serving as CEO, he held several senior leadership roles at Intuit, including Executive Vice President and General Manager of both the Small Business Group and the Consumer Tax Group. Earlier in his career, he served as Global President of the Products Group at Invensys and held senior leadership positions at Honeywell. Drawing on decades of leadership across global organizations, Mr. Goodarzi brings significant operational and management expertise from guiding large-scale businesses through growth and transformation.
