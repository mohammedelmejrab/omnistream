Item 5.02

Departure of Directors or Certain Officers; Election of Directors; Appointment of Certain Officers; Compensatory Arrangements of Certain Officers.

(b) On October 19, 2026, Richard Rothberg, Executive Vice President, General Counsel and Secretary of Dell Technologies Inc. (“the Company”), notified the Company of his decision to retire from his position, in which position he served as the Company’s chief legal officer, effective as of February 1, 2027. Mr. Rothberg will remain employed by the Company to provide transitional assistance through April 30, 2027.

On October 20, 2026, the Board of Directors appointed Christopher Garcia, who currently serves as the Company’s Senior Vice President, Corporate Legal, and Assistant Secretary of the Company, to succeed Mr. Rothberg in that position and as the Company’s chief legal officer, effective as of February 1, 2027. Mr. Garcia has served as Senior Vice President, Corporate Legal, and Assistant Secretary of the Company since 2022, where he has responsibility for corporate governance and regulatory compliance. Since joining the Company in 2006, Mr. Garcia has served in various global legal leadership roles, including international, commercial, and product and services development. Before beginning his service with the Company, Mr. Garcia worked in private practice at the law firms of Fenwick & West LLP in Mountain View, California and Clifford Chance US LLP in San Francisco, California.
