October 29, 2026 Federal Reserve Board announces approval of application by The Bank of Nova Scotia For release at 4:30 p.m. EDT Share The Federal Reserve Board on Thursday announced its approval of the application by The Bank of Nova Scotia, of Toronto, Canada, to become a bank holding company by acquiring Maple Financial Holdings, Inc., of Dallas, Texas, and thereby indirectly acquiring MapleMark Bank, of Tulsa, Oklahoma.
