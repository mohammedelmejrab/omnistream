October 16, 2026

Federal Reserve Board announces termination of enforcement actions with Gardner Bancshares, Inc. and Small Business Bank

For release at 11:00 a.m. EDT

  • Share

The Federal Reserve Board on Friday announced the termination of the enforcement actions listed below:

Gardner Bancshares, Inc., Lenexa, Kansas and Small Business Bank, Lenexa, Kansas
Cease and Desist Order dated September 1, 2023 (PDF)
Terminated October 13, 2026

Small Business Bank, Lenexa, Kansas
Cease and Desist Order dated November 13, 2024 (PDF)
Terminated October 13, 2026

Small Business Bank, Lenexa, Kansas
Prompt Corrective Action dated June 29, 2026 (PDF)
Terminated October 13, 2026
