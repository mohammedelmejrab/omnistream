Samsung Electronics , a Worldwide Olympic and Paralympic Partner, today announced that it is bringing Galaxy technology and athlete-centered programming to the Youth Olympic Games Dakar 2026 to enrich the experience of young athletes during an important stage in their Olympic journeys. Taking place in Senegal from Oct. 31 to Nov. 13, Dakar 2026 is bringing together more than 2,700 young athletes from 207 countries across 25 sports. It also marks a historic milestone as the first Olympic sporting event to be held on the African continent. (Copyright: IOC/Franco Arland) Helping Power the Youth Olympic Games: Bringing Galaxy Innovation to Dakar 2026 Having introduced sports technology integrations at Paris 2024 and Milano Cortina 2026, Samsung is now bringing these innovations to the Youth Olympic Games for the first time. Galaxy S26 Ultra devices are being incorporated into skateboarding and taekwondo coverage alongside existing broadcast cameras, enabling handheld, close-up, and vertical perspectives that can complement traditional production. Dakar 2026 is also serving as an opportunity to test and learn from these applications as Samsung continues exploring how mobile technology can support future Games experiences — including on the road to the LA28 Games. The Olympic Victory Selfie is making its Youth Olympic Games debut at Dakar 2026, extending a program that has become an iconic expression of athlete celebration at the Olympic and Paralympic Games since its debut at Paris 2024. Centered on connection, emotion, and self-expression, Victory Selfie gives athletes a more personal way to capture and share the moments that define their journey. Using Galaxy Z Flip7 Olympic Edition devices, medalists across disciplines will have the opportunity to commemorate their winning moments as part of Victory Selfie activations around medal ceremonies to create lasting memories of their achievement that are sharable with friends, family, and supporters around the world. ▲ International Olympic Committee (IOC) President Kirsty Coventry stops by the Samsung Open Station for a Victory Selfie with Team Senegal athletes Woreye Ndiaye (Wushu), Ibrahima Beye (Taekwondo), Naël Lamine Voisin (Swimming), Ndèye Khady Anna Diatta (Beach Wrestling), and Team Korea athletes Jang Yu Jeong (Badminton) and Park Jung Bin (Badminton). (Copyright: IOC/Ubald Rutar) Samsung’s Commitment to the Olympic Games: Bringing Dakar 2026 Closer to Local Communities Dakar 2026 marks a milestone as both the first Olympic sporting event held on the African continent and as a new chapter in Samsung’s support of the Youth Olympic Games, which dates back to Singapore 2010. Alongside its athlete-facing programs, Samsung is extending the excitement of the Games into communities across Senegal through local media, digital, retail, and consumer activations designed to help more people take part in the excitement of Dakar 2026. Out-of-home campaigns will spotlight Dakar 2026 — and Samsung’s programming — across key locations in Dakar and Senegal while Samsung consumer experience zones will bring the excitement of the Youth Olympic Games into local communities. This will give fans opportunities to explore Galaxy technology, take part in interactive sports and AI-powered challenges, and create their own Olympic-inspired moments. Samsung is also working with domestic partners on their retail initiatives for Dakar 2026, including special Galaxy offers and in-store activations, while a citywide pin program spanning Samsung locations will encourage consumers to explore the Games and participate in Samsung experiences throughout Dakar. Together, these programs extend Samsung’s commitment to the Olympic Games beyond the competition venues — supporting young athletes at the center of the Games while creating more ways for local fans and communities to experience and celebrate this historic moment. About Samsung’s Involvement in the Olympic Games Samsung has been a Worldwide Olympic Partner since the Olympic Winter Games Nagano 1998. For over 25 years, athletes and fans have trusted Samsung’s transformative mobile technology to share the Olympic spirit globally and help to shape the digital future of the Olympic Games. The company’s commitment to the Olympic Movement soon faces its fourth decade of partnership and extends through the LA28 Games. Samsung’s purposeful innovations in the wireless communications and computing equipment category, including equipment that features artificial intelligence, virtual reality, augmented reality, and 5G will help to change the way the world experiences the Olympic Games. About Samsung’s Involvement in the Youth Olympic Games Samsung has supported the Youth Olympic Games since the inaugural Singapore 2010 Youth Olympic Games, accompanying young athletes as they take on new challenges and begin their Olympic journeys. At Dakar 2026, Samsung continues that commitment by using Galaxy technology and athlete-focused experiences to help the next generation connect, create, and share their stories on the Olympic stage.
