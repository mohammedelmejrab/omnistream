Samsung Electronics opened its “Design is an Act of Love” exhibition on October 15 at Dongdaemun Design Plaza (DDP) in Seoul as part of Seoul Design Week 2026, one of Korea’s leading design events running through October 25. Samsung collaborated with Italian design education institution POLI.design and 14 teams of emerging Italian artists to present artworks celebrating human diversity and individuality. Inspired by Samsung products, the 14 pieces reflect the company’s design vision rooted in understanding and caring for people. Each is displayed on a Samsung OLED TV (model S95H) with a custom-designed frame. ▲ Artworks displayed on Samsung OLED TVs are complemented by custom-designed frames. Looking ahead, the company aims to push creative boundaries by bringing artistic imagination and technology together in new ways.
