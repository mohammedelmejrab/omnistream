NVIDIA DSX AI Factory Platform brings together technologies to help the ecosystem design, build, and operate more efficient AI factories, including open source, modular software components built to support multi-tenant operations and scaling. Operating an AI factory requires a shared understanding of distributed workloads: their components, pod templates, replica counts, and status.
