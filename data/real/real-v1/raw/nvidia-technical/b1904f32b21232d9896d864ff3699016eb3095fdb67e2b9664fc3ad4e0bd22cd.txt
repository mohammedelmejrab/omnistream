As teams deploy and scale AI workloads using NVIDIA Dynamo, Kubeflow, or Slurm, tracking what actually runs requires visibility beyond the platform configuration captured in a recipe. Application teams define and update these workloads independently of the platform recipe, so a reproducible deployment bundle alone cannot establish which workload artifacts are running on each node.
