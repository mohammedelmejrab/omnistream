AI is expanding beyond the data center as developers build a new generation of agents to act at the edge: monitoring driver attention and cabin conditions, helping robots understand their surroundings and plan tasks, and coordinating fleets of machines on production lines. These physical AI applications rely on models that perceive, reason, and act within tight memory, power, and latency budgets.
