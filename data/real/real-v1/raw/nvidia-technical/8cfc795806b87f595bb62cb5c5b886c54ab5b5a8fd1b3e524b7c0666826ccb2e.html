Many AI workloads need only a fraction of a GPU. Giving each one an entire device can leave capacity idle; sharing GPUs lets platform teams serve more workloads with the infrastructure they already have. NVIDIA GPUs already support several approaches to sharing. Multi-instance GPU (MIG) splits a GPU into hardware-isolated instances that are transparent to the application…
