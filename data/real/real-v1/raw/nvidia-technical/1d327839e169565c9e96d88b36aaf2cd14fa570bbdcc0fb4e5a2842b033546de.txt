A Siemens report estimates that unplanned downtime costs an average large plant in the surveyed sectors $253 million per year. Whether investigating a failed circuit board or responding to a rush order, factory teams must gather evidence across systems, consult specialists, and coordinate the next step. The data may be available, but turning it into a response takes substantial time and effort. Source
