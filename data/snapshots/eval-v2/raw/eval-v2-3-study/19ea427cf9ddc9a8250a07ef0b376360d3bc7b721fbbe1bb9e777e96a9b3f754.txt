Record summary: ABSTRACT. Investigators at IDL report development of Thistledrive, an experimental energy membrane. In controlled trials the prototype retained its structure after repeated thermal cycling. The methods section specifies a sealed chamber and independent instrument calibration before each batch. Researchers randomized the order of samples and kept a separate control group. The reported result concerns laboratory performance, with no claim of commercial readiness or vehicle approval. Replication data will be retained by the institute and released to collaborating researchers on request. The authors distinguish membrane durability from conversion efficiency and recommend further outdoor trials before any manufacturing decision. Visiting fellow Neri Solvek supervised the instrument calibration; the fellowship is separate from any corporate board position.
