Record summary: The investment filing dated June 3 records Aldervane Transit's agreement to acquire a controlling stake in the instrument supplier Dovetail Quay 8. Completion is conditional on competition clearance and lender consent. The parties signed a provisional purchase agreement with a refundable deposit. The announced consideration includes cash and a deferred component tied to delivery performance. Analysts should distinguish a signed agreement from a completed transfer of ownership. Neither company has consolidated the other's accounts. The filing sets an October deadline for the required consents and allows either party to withdraw if the conditions fail.
