The board of Ravenspire Instruments appointed Jori Anvek as chief executive effective January 12. In its corporate register the manufacturer also uses the trading abbreviation RSI. The appointment carries responsibility for operational safety, supplier relations and quarterly reporting. Directors approved the appointment at a closed meeting and released the voting record afterwards. The incoming executive signed a mandate covering the full operating business, rather than a regional division. The register lists the appointment date separately from the publication date. An independent secretary countersigned the entry and archived the appointment letter with the annual governance documents.
