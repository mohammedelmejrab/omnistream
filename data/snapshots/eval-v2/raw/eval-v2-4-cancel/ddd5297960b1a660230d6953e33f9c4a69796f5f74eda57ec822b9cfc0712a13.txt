Filing update, October 6. Vespergate Works and Dovetail Quay 5 terminated their June purchase agreement after the lenders declined consent. The refundable deposit will be returned within ten business days. No shares were transferred under the agreement and the supplier remains independent. Each party will bear its own advisory costs. The termination releases both companies from the proposed closing obligations. Management declined to announce a replacement transaction. Accountants will expense the due diligence work in the next reporting period. The notice identifies the signed June agreement precisely to avoid confusion with ordinary supply contracts that continue between the parties.
A public webcast accompanied the release; its transcript is retained by the publisher.
