DATA DEPOSIT / November 2. The CFI team deposited the replication archive for Rillmesh under accession SM-91. The archive includes instrument settings, anonymized batch measurements and a description of excluded samples. A curator checked that the package could be opened independently of the authors' laboratory software. Readers should use the accession identifier when requesting this particular deposit. The archive is released for replication studies and does not certify commercial product performance. A checksum accompanies each file to detect transmission errors. The institute will preserve earlier versions alongside any corrected uploads so that published analyses remain reproducible.
A public webcast accompanied the release; its transcript is retained by the publisher.
