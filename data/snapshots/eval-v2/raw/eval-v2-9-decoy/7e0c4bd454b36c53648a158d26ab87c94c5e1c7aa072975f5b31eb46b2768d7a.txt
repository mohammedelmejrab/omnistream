INTERVIEW. A spokesperson for Ravenspear Insurance rejected suggestions that the firm financed Silver Marsh Laboratory. "Our similarly named neighbor operates in a different industry," the spokesperson said. "Our September product inspection did not lead to an executive resignation." The firm's expenditure review covered training and office systems, with no research grant to the named institution. An editor checked the registration record and confirmed that the business is legally separate from Ravenspire Instruments. The distinction matters because an early market newsletter confused the two businesses. The correction preserves the separate trading names and warns readers against combining their financial statements.
