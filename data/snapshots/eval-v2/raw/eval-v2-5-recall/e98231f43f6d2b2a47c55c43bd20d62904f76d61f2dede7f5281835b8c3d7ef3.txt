SAFETY WIRE / September 9. Quenara Devices recalled its vehicle modules after inspectors identified a defective steering pin. The chief executive resigned following the recall on September 11. The notice refers to that officer as the QND chief and does not name a replacement. Shipments from the affected assembly line were suspended pending inspection. Customers were offered replacement parts and a dedicated telephone service. The board established a safety committee to examine the supplier records. Directors said that the resignation and the recall were separate resolutions adopted during the same emergency meeting. Export deliveries remained suspended through the weekend.
