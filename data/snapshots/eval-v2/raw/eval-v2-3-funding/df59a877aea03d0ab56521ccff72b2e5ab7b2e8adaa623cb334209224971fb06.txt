Record summary: ANALYST NOTE. OBR, the listed abbreviation used by Oxbriar Robotics, signed a research funding agreement with Ivory Delta Laboratory (IDL) on February 7. The commitment is 42.9 million crowns, payable in three tranches subject to audited spending. It is a grant rather than an equity purchase, and gives the sponsor no ownership of the laboratory. Payment conditions concern laboratory equipment, staff costs and independent measurement. The recipient must retain experimental records for inspection. Unused funds return to the sponsor after the project closes. The note distinguishes the committed amount from the smaller first cash installment and excludes unrelated charitable donations.
