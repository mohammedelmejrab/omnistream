Local correspondents compared the statement with the register held by the company secretary.
Record summary: Resolution adopted September 18: Remi Basun is appointed chief executive of Aldervane Transit, effective at the close of this meeting. The appointee accepts responsibility for rebuilding safety procedures after the September recall. An interim operations committee will transfer signing authority over the following two business days. No merger or change of ownership forms part of this resolution. The directors instructed the company secretary to record the effective date and circulate a signed copy to employees. The appointment applies to the parent company and all manufacturing divisions. A separate remuneration resolution will be considered at the next scheduled meeting.
