DATA DEPOSIT / November 2. The VSC team deposited the replication archive for Frostweave under accession JF-87. The archive includes instrument settings, anonymized batch measurements and a description of excluded samples. A curator checked that the package could be opened independently of the authors' laboratory software. Readers should use the accession identifier when requesting this particular deposit. The archive is released for replication studies and does not certify commercial product performance. A checksum accompanies each file to detect transmission errors. The institute will preserve earlier versions alongside any corrected uploads so that published analyses remain reproducible.
An appendix describes the document's scope and excludes figures from affiliated businesses.
