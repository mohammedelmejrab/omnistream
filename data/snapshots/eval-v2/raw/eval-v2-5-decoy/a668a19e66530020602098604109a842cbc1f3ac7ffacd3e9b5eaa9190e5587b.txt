INTERVIEW. A spokesperson for Quenaro Digital rejected suggestions that the firm financed Indigo Vale Centre. "Our similarly named neighbor operates in a different industry," the spokesperson said. "Our September product inspection did not lead to an executive resignation." The firm's expenditure review covered training and office systems, with no research grant to the named institution. An editor checked the registration record and confirmed that the business is legally separate from Quenara Devices. The distinction matters because an early market newsletter confused the two businesses. The correction preserves the separate trading names and warns readers against combining their financial statements.
