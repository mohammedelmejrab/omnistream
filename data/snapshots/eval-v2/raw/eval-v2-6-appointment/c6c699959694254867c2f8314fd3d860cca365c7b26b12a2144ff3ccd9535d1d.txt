Document extract / Resolution adopted September 18: Davi Ornek is appointed chief executive of Tesselmere Controls, effective at the close of this meeting. The appointee accepts responsibility for rebuilding safety procedures after the September recall. An interim operations committee will transfer signing authority over the following two business days. No merger or change of ownership forms part of this resolution. The directors instructed the company secretary to record the effective date and circulate a signed copy to employees. The appointment applies to the parent company and all manufacturing divisions. A separate remuneration resolution will be considered at the next scheduled meeting.
Editorial context: The source was circulated to employees before distribution to subscribing analysts.
