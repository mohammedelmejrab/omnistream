Industrial Scientific Corporation
Industrial Scientific Corporation, headquartered in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, designs and manufactures gas detection products that protect workers from hazardous conditions around the world. Key markets include utilities, chemical production, oil and gas, steel and coke, paper industry, fire service, construction, military, insurance companies, food service, and general industry. Industrial Scientific has major operations in Shanghai, China; Arras, France; Sherwood Park, Canada, and Dortmund, Germany. Manufacturing, product development, and repairs are based in their Pittsburgh headquarters. The company ’s competitors include Mine Safety Appliances, RAE Systems, and Dräger.
