Prince Adarnase of Kartli
Afanasy Bagration ("At'anase BagrationiAfanasiy Leonovich Bagration"), born Adarnase (ადარნასე) (15 November 1707 – 31 March 1784) was a Georgian prince royal ("batonishvili") of the Bagrationi dynasty of House of Mukhrani of Kartli and a natural son of Levan of Kartli by a concubine. He followed, in 1724, his half-brother King Vakhtang VI of Kartli in an exile to the Russian Empire, where Afanasy pursued a military career and attained to the rank of general poruchik.
