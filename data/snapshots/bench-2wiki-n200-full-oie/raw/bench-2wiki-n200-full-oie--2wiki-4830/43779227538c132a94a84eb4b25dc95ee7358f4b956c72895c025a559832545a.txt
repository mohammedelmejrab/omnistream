Carolyn Brandt
Carolyn Brandt is an American actress and dancer. She was the wife of cult film director Ray Dennis Steckler and starred in many of his films, including" The Incredibly Strange Creatures Who Stopped Living and Became Mixed- Up Zombies !!? The Thrill Killers", and" Rat Pfink a Boo Boo". Brandt was featured in the film" It's a Bikini World" as the dancer in" Liar, Liar" with The Castaways.
