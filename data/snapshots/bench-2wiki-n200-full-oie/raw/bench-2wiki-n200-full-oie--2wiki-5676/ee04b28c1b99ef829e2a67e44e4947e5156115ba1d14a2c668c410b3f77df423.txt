Dibyasambandh
Princess Dibyasambanna (Née Her Serene Highness Princess (Mom Chao) Dibyasambanna Bhanubandh ; ; 17 May 1885 - 26 July 1908) was a Princess of Siam (later Thailand). She was a member of Siamese royal family, specifically the Bhanubandh family, as a daughter of Prince Bhanurangsi Savangwongse and Mom Liam.
