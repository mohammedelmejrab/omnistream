John II of Brienne, Count of Eu
John II of Brienne( died 11 July 1302 in Kortrijk) was the son of John I of Brienne, Count of Eu and Beatrice of Saint- Pol. He succeeded his father as Count of Eu in 1294. He married Jeanne, Countess of Guînes( d. 1331/ 32), the daughter and heir of Baldwin IV, Count of Guînes. They had two children: John was killed at the Battle of the Golden Spurs.
