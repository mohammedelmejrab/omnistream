La mazurka del barone, della santa e del fico fiorone
La mazurka del barone, della santa e del fico fiorone( also known as" The Mazurka of the Baron, the Saint and the Early Fig Tree") is a 1975 Italian comedy film directed by Pupi Avati. It is considered one of the most atypical commedia all'italiana films. The film marked Avati's return to filmmaking after a six- year absence, during which he worked as director of TV commercials.
