Jan II the Mad
Jan II the Mad also known as the Bad, the Wild or the Cruel( 16 April 1435 – 22 September 1504), was a Duke of Żagań- Przewóz since 1439( with his brothers as co-rulers until 1449), from 1449 Duke of Przewóz( as co-ruler of his younger brother), during 1461 – 1468 and briefly in 1472 Duke of Żagań and during 1476 – 1488 Duke of half- Głogów( the Duchy was finally reunited in 1480). He was the fourth and youngest son of Duke Jan I of Żagań by his wife Scholastika, daughter of Rudolf III, Duke of Saxe- Wittenberg and Elector of Saxony.
