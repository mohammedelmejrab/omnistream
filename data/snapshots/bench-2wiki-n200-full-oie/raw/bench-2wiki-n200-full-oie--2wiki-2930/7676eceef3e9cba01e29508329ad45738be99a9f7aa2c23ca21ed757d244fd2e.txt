Grand Duchess Elena Pavlovna of Russia
Grand Duchess Elena Pavlovna of Russia, Hereditary Grand Duchess of Mecklenburg( 24 December 1784[ OS 13 December] – 24 September 1803) was a daughter of Grand Duke, later Tsar Paul I of Russia and his second wife Sophie Dorothea of Württemberg. After marrying the son and heir of the Grand Duke of Mecklenburg- Schwerin she ceased to use her Russian title.
