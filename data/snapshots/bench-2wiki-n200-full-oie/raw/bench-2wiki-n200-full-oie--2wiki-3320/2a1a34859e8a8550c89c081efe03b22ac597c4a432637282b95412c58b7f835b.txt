Pacific Rendezvous
Pacific Rendezvous is a 1942 film directed by George Sidney. It stars Lee Bowman and Jean Rogers. The previous film version was the 1935 film" Rendezvous", starring William Powell and Rosalind Russell, directed by William K. Howard and Sam Wood. P. J. Wolfson and George Oppenheimer were credited with the screenplay of" Rendezvous", and Bella Spewack and Samuel Spewack were credited as adapters of the book" The American Black Chamber" by Major Herbert O. Yardley( 1931).
