4 Minute Mile
4 Minute Mile is a 2014 drama film directed by Charles- Olivier Michaud, written by Josh Campbell and Jeff Van Wie, and starring Richard Jenkins, Kelly Blatz, Analeigh Tipton, Kim Basinger, and Cam Gigandet. It is distributed by Gravitas Ventures, and the international sales rights are held by Double Dutch International. The film premiered at the Seattle International Film Festival( SIFF) on June 5, 2014. Gravitas planned for a theatrical release on August 1, 2014.
