Doris Wishman
Doris Wishman( June 1, 1912 August 10, 2002) was an American film director, screenwriter and producer. She is credited with having directed and produced at least thirty feature films during a career spanning over four decades, most notably in the sexploitation film genre. A native of New York City, Wishman began her film career as a hobby after the death of her husband in 1958. She made her feature debut with" Hideout in the Sun"( 1960), and went on to direct numerous nudist and sexploitation films, such as" Gentlemen Prefer Nature Girls"( 1963)," Behind the Nudist Curtain"( 1963), and" Bad Girls Go to Hell"( 1965). In the 1970s, she would make her first foray into directing pornographic films. In 1979, Wishman filmed her first and only feature horror film," A Night to Dismember", which she spent several years editing after multiple reels were destroyed during post-production. She would make a further three films in the early 2000s before dying in 2002, aged 90.
