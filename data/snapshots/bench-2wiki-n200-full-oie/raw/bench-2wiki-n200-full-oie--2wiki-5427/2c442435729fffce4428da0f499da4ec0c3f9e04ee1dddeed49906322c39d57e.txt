William Clemens (film director)
William Clemens( September 10, 1905 – April 29, 1980) was an American film director. Born in Saginaw, Michigan, Clemens began his Hollywood career as a film editor in 1931. His first directing project was" Man Hunt" in 1936. His major credits include" On Dress Parade" with the Dead End Kids, two Perry Mason mysteries(" The Case of the Velvet Claws" and" The Case of the Stuttering Bishop"), three films featuring detective Tom Lawrence, a.k.a. " The Falcon," four films based on the Nancy Drew series, and" Calling Philo Vance" in 1940. Clemens' final project was" The Thirteenth Hour" in 1947. He died in Los Angeles, California in 1980.
