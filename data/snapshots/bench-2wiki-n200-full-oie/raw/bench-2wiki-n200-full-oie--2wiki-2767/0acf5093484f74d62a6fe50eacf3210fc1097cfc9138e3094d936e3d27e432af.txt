Nell Campbell
Laura Elizabeth Campbell( born 24 May 1953), known professionally as Nell Campbell or Little Nell, is an Australian actress, singer and club owner. She is best known for her role as Columbia in the film" The Rocky Horror Picture Show"( 1975), and the original stage play from which it was adapted. Campbell released her debut EP," The Musical World of Little Nell( Aquatic Teenage Sex& Squalor)," in 1978 by A&M Records. She appeared as Nurse Ansalong in the 1981 film" Shock Treatment".
