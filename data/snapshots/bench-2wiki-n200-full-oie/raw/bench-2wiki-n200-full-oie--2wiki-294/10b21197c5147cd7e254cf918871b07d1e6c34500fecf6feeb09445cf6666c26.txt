Ven a bailar conmigo
" Ven a bailar conmigo"( English:" Come dance with me") was the Norwegian entry in the Eurovision Song Contest 2007, performed in English and Spanish by Guri Schanke. The song is a Latin- inspired number. Schanke sings about how much she loves to dance and urges her unnamed listener( in Spanish) to" Come dance with me". BBC commentary during the Contest explained that Schanke had participated in a reality- television show with a dancing theme, thus explaining the lyrics. The song was written and composed by, who also contributed the Spanish entry at the same Contest-" I Love You Mi Vida". This represents one of a very small number of Contests where the same writer has represented two countries at once.
