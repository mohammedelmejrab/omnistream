Rudolf of Żagań
Rudolf of Żagań( ca. 1418 – 18 September 1454) was a Duke of Żagań- Przewóz since 1439( with his brothers as co-rulers until 1449), from 1449 Duke of Żagań( as co-ruler of his older brother). He was the second son of Duke Jan I of Żagań by his wife Scholastika, daughter of Rudolf III, Duke of Saxe- Wittemberg and Elector of Saxony. He was named after his maternal grandfather.
