Henry IV, Prince of Anhalt-Bernburg
Henry IV, Prince of Anhalt- Bernburg( died 7 July 1374) was a German prince of the House of Ascania and ruler of the principality of Anhalt- Bernburg. He was the second son of Bernhard III, Prince of Anhalt- Bernburg, by his first wife Agnes, daughter of Rudolf I, Duke of Saxe- Wittenberg.
