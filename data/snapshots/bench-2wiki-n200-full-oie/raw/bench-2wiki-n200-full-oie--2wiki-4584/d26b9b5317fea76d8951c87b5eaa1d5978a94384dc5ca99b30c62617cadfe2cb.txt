Little Ol' Cowgirl
Little Ol' Cowgirl is the second studio album by American country band Dixie Chicks, released in 1992( see 1992 in music). As with their previous album, it produced no chart singles. It was also the last album to feature Robin Lynn Macy, who left in late 1992 over a dispute with the Erwin sisters over the musical direction of the band. The song" Past the Point of Rescue" was also recorded by Hal Ketchum on his 1991 album" Past the Point of Rescue", from which it was released as a single in 1992.
