Nemai Ghosh (photographer)
Nemai Ghosh( born 1934) is a noted Indian photographer most known for working with Satyajit Ray, as a still photographer for over two decades, starting with" Goopy Gyne Bagha Byne"( 1969) till Ray's last film" Agantuk"( 1991). He was a jury member at the 2007 National Film Awards, and was awarded the Padma Shri by Government of India in 2010.
