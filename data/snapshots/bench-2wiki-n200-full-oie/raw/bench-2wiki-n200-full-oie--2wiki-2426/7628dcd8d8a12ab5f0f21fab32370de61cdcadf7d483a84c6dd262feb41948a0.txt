Clancy of the Mounted
Clancy of the Mounted( 1933) is an American pre-Code Universal movie serial based on the poem by Robert W. Service, directed by Ray Taylor. Tom Tyler played Sgt. Clancy, and William L. Thorne played the villainous claim jumper, Black McDougal. This was Universal's 85th serial and the 17th with sound. Though long considered lost, the first six chapters were released on DVD by Serialsquadron.com in 2012; According to MDB the British Film Institute( BFI) holds a 35 mm print of all 12 chapters in completion
