Mane Devru
Mane Devru is a 1993 Indian Kannada language romantic drama film directed and enacted by V. Ravichandran along with Sudharani pairing together for the first time. The supporting cast features K. S. Ashwath, Tennis Krishna, Disco Shanti among others. The film was a Hit at the box office. The film was a remake of K. Bhagyaraj's Tamil blockbuster film" Mouna Geethangal"( 1981) and was also remade in Hindi as" Ek Hi Bhool"( 1981).
