Princess Maria-Olympia of Greece and Denmark
Princess Maria- Olympia of Greece and Denmark( born 25 July 1996) is an American fashion model, socialite and member of the Greek royal family. She is the oldest child and only daughter of Pavlos, Crown Prince of Greece and his wife, Marie- Chantal Miller. Her paternal grandparents are Constantine II of Greece and Anne- Marie of Denmark, who were the last King and Queen of the Hellenes, while her maternal grandfather is duty free entrepreneur Robert Warren Miller. She is currently sixth in the line of succession to the former throne of Greece, after her father and brothers.
