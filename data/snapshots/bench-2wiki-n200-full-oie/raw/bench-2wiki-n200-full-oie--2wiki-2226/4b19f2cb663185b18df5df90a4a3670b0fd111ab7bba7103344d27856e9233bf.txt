The Strawberry Blonde
The Strawberry Blonde is a 1941 American romantic comedy film directed by Raoul Walsh, starring James Cagney and Olivia de Havilland, and featuring Rita Hayworth, Alan Hale, Jack Carson, and George Tobias. The picture was nominated for an Academy Award in 1941 for Best Scoring of a Musical Picture and features songs such as" The Band Played OnBill Bailey Meet Me in St. Louis, Louie, Wait Till The Sun Shines Nellie," and" Love Me and the World Is Mine." The title is most often listed beginning with the word The, but the film's posters and promotional materials call it simply Strawberry Blonde. Director Walsh remade the film in 1948 as" One Sunday Afternoon". It was a remake of" One Sunday Afternoon"( 1933) with Gary Cooper.
