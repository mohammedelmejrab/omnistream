John Murray (Irish broadcaster)
John Murray is an Irish journalist and broadcaster. Currently heard on Weekend Sport, he presented" The John Murray Show" on RTÉ Radio 1," focus[ ing] on lifestyle and entertainment items", filling the slot from 09:00 to 10:00 previously occupied by Ryan Tubridy, who moved to RTÉ 2 fm. Murray was one of the presenters of" Morning Ireland", Ireland's most popular radio show, before going on to host his own weekly programme," The Business".
