Reynaldo Aguinaldo
Reynaldo B. Aguinaldo( born September 28, 1946 – March 24, 2019) was a Filipino politician who served as mayor of Kawit, Cavite from 2007 to 2016. He had previously served as vice mayor for three consecutive terms, from 1998 to 2007. Aguinaldo was a grandson( son of Emilio Aguinaldo, Jr) of Emilio Aguinaldo, the first President of the Philippines. He died from a cardiac arrest on March 24, 2019.
