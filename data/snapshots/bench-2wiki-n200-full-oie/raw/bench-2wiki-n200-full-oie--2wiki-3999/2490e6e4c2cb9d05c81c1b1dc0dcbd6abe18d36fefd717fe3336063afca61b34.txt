Larissa França
Larissa França Maestrini( born April 14, 1982 in Cachoeiro de Itapemirim) is a beach volleyball player from Brazil. She is the all- time leader of beach volleyball titles, with 57 FIVB career gold medals, including the 2011 Beach Volleyball World Championships with Juliana Felisberta and the 2015 FIVB Beach Volleyball World Tour with Talita Antunes.
