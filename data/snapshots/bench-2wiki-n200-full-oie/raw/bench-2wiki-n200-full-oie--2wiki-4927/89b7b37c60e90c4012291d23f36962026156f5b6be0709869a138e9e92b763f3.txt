The Keeper of the Bees (1925 film)
The Keeper of the Bees is a 1925 American drama film written and directed by James Leo Meehan. It is based on the 1925 novel" The Keeper of the Bees" by Gene Stratton Porter. The film stars Robert Frazer, Josef Swickard, Martha Mattox, Clara Bow, Alyce Mills and Gene Stratton. The film was released on September 6, 1925, by Film Booking Offices of America.
