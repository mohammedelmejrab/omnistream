Free Hand for a Tough Cop
Free Hand for a Tough Cop, also known as" Tough Cop", is an Italian poliziottesco-action film directed in 1976 by Umberto Lenzi. In this movie Tomas Milian plays for the first time Sergio Marazzi a.k.a." Er Monnezza", a role that he later played several more times, in Lenzi's" Brothers Till We Die"( 1978, a sort of sequel of this movie), in" Destruction Force" by Stelvio Massi( 1977) and, with slight differences, in" Uno contro l'altro, praticamente amici" by Bruno Corbucci( 1980) and in Francesco Massaro's" Il lupo e l'agnello"( 1980).
