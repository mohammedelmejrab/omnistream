Lucius Antonius Albus (proconsul of Asia)
Lucius Antonius Albus was a Roman senator of the 2nd century AD who occupied a number of offices in the imperial service, as well as serving as suffect consul" circa" 132. Albus is best known for his tenure as proconsular governor of Asia, when, according to Aelius Aristides, a series of earthquakes struck western Asia Minor. Albus has been identified as the son of the homonymous consul of 102, and Laevia Paulla; his great- grandfather was a priest of Artemis in Ephesus.
