Archduchess Maria Antonia of Austria (1858–1883)
Archduchess Maria Antonia of Austria, Princess of Tuscany ("Maria Antonia Leopoldina Annunciata Anna Amalia Josepha Johanna Thelka"; 10 January 1858 – 13 April 1883) was a member of the House of Habsburg-Lorraine. She served as the Princess-Abbess of the Theresian Royal and Imperial Convent in Hradčany from 1881 until her death in 1883.
