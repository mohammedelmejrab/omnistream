Vitti Dandu
Vitti Dandu( Marathi :विटी दांडू) is a first Marathi- language film produced by Ajay Devgn. Film is directed by Ganesh Kadam and written by Vikas Kadam. The film stars Dilip Prabhavalkar, Ashok Samarth In lead roles. Trailer of the film was launched on YouTube on 14 August 2014, one day before the Indian Independence Day.
