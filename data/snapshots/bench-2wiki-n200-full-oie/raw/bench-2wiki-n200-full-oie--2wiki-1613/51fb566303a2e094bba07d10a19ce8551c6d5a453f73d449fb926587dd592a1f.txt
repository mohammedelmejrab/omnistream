Umar II
Umar ibn Abd al- Aziz( 2 November 682 –), commonly known as Umar II, was the eighth Umayyad caliph, ruling from 22 September 717 until his death in 720. He was also a cousin of the former caliph, being the son of Abd al- Malik's younger brother, Abd al- Aziz. He was also a matrilineal great- grandson of the second caliph, Umar ibn Al- Khattab.
