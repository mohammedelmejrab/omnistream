William Alington, 1st Baron Alington
William Alington, 1st Baron Alington of Killard( baptised 14 March 1610/ 11 – buried 25 October 1648) was an Irish peer, the son of Sir Giles Alington. He was created 1st Baron Alington of Killard, on 28 July 1642. He married Elizabeth Tollemache, daughter of Sir Lionel Tollemache, 2nd Baronet and Elizabeth Stanhope, before 1 October 1631. They had at least 6 children: After his death his widow, Lady Alington, remarried Sir William Compton, who died in 1663. She died in 1671.
