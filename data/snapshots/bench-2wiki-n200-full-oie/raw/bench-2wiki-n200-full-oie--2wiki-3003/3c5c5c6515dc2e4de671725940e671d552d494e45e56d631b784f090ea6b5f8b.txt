Thayagam
Thayagam is a 1996 Tamil action film directed by A. R. Ramesh. The film features Vijayakanth, Arun Pandian and Napoleon in lead roles. The film had musical score by Deva and was released on 15 January 1996. The film did not do well at the box office. It was dubbed in Telugu as" Maathru Bhoomi". Vijayakanth won Tamil Nadu State Film Award Special Prize for this film.
