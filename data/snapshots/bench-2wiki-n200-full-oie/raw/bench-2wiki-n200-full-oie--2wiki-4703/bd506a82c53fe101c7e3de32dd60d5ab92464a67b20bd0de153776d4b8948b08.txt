Anthony Scott Burns
Anthony Scott Burns( born May 9, 1977) is a Canadian filmmaker, visual effects artist and musician. After starting his film career as a visual effects artist and director, as of 2015 he is directing three feature films in pre-production:" Dark MatterHolidays", and Plan B Entertainment's" Alpha". He also releases music under the moniker Pilotpriest. Our House is currently in post and should be released in 2018.
