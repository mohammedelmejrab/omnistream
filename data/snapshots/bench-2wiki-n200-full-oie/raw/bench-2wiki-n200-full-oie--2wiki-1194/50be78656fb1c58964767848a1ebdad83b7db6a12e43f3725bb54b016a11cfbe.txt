Al-Abbas ibn al-Ma'mun
Al- Abbas ibn al- Ma'mun( died 838 CE) was an Abbasid prince and general, the son of the Abbasid Caliph al- Ma'mun. A distinguished military leader in the Arab – Byzantine wars, he was passed over in the succession in favour of his uncle al- Mu'tasim. In 838, he was arrested for his involvement in a failed conspiracy against al- Mu'tasim, and died in prison.
