Moguds Pellams
Moguds Pellams is a 2005 Telugu film directed by actor Ranganath who made his directorial debut and was his only directorial so far. The film stars Sivaji Raja, Rathi in the lead roles. Two strangers meet each other while traveling from their native place to find a job in the city. To find an accommodation, they pretend to be a married couple. With time, they are attracted to each other, but are hesitant to admit it but finally unites.
