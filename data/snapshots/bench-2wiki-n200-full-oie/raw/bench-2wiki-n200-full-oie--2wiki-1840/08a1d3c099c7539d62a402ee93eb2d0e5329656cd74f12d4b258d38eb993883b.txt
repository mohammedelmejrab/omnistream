Anna of Pomerania, Duchess of Lubin
Anna of Pomerania( 1492 – 25 April 1550) was a German Princess. She was a member of the House of Pomerania( also known as House of Greifen or House of Griffins) and by marriage Duchess of Brzeg. She was the eldest daughter of Bogislaw X, Duke of Pomerania, by his second wife Anna, daughter of King Casimir IV of Poland.
