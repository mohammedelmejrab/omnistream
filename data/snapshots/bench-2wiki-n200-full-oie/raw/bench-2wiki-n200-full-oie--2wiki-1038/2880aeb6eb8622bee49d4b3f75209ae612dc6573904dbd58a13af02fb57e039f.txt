Nakkhatra Mangala
Nakkhatra Mangala, Prince of Chanthaburi II (4 January 1897 – 11 February 1953), was the eldest son of Kitiyakara Voralaksana, Prince of Chanthaburi I and Princess Apsarasaman Kitiyakara.
