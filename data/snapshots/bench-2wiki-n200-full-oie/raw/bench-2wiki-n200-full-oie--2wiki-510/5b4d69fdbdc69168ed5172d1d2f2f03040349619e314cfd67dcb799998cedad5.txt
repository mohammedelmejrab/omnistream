Gare de Montreuil-Bellay
Gare de Montreuil- Bellay is the main railway station serving the village of Montreuil- Bellay, Maine- et- Loire, western France. It is situated on the Saumur- Thouars railway, an electrified branch line of approximately 10 miles in length. To the north of the line Gare de Saumur also lies on the Tours – Saint- Nazaire railway. TGV services to Paris are available from Tours. Gare de Thouars to the south is situated on the Loudun- La Roche- Sur- Yon railway.
