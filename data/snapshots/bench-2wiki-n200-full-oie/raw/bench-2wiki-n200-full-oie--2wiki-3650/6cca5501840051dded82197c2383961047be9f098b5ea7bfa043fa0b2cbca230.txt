A Lizard in a Woman's Skin
A Lizard in a Woman's Skin( Italian:" Una lucertola con la pelle di donna") is a 1971 Italian giallo film directed by Lucio Fulci. The film was released in France as" Carole", and was later re-released in the US as" Schizoid"( hardcore version, the same as the French version" Les Salopes vont en Enfer", released in 1976). Set in London, the film follows Carol Hammond( Florinda Bolkan), the daughter of a respected politician, who experiences a series of vivid, psychedelic nightmares consisting of depraved sex orgies and LSD use. In the dream, she commits a graphic murder and awakes to a real- life criminal investigation into the murder of her neighbour.
