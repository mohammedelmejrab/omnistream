S Durga
Sexy Durga( Changed into S Durga for Indian release) is a 2017 Indian Malayalam- language indie drama film directed by Sanal Kumar Sasidharan. The film is about the religious divide in the society of Kerala and its effect on marriage between two people from two different religious backgrounds. It has won the Hivos Tiger Award at the 2017 International Film Festival Rotterdam, and the Golden Apricot at Armenia's Yerevan International Film Festival in the category International Feature Competition, 2017. It is the only Indian film to win that title. It has also won the Best International Feature Film award at the Guanajuato International Film Festival in Mexico.
