Christian, Duke of Saxe-Weissenfels
Christian, Duke of Saxe- Weissenfels( 23 February 1682 in Weissenfels – 28 June 1736 in Sangerhausen), was a duke of Saxe- Weissenfels- Querfurt and member of the House of Wettin. He was the sixth( but second surviving) son of Johann Adolf I, Duke of Saxe- Weissenfels, and Johanna Magdalena of Saxe- Altenburg.
