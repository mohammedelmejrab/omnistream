Léonide Moguy
Léonide Moguy( 14 July 1899 – 21 April 1976) was a Russian born French film director, screenwriter and film editor. Moguy was born Leonid Mogilevsky in Russia in 1899. He lived in Russia until 1928, in the United States in the 1940s, and in Italy from 1949 until his death. He was active in film between 1936 and 1961. His work has influenced American director Quentin Tarantino, who discovered him while writing the script for" Inglourious Basterds", and named a character after him in" Django Unchained".
