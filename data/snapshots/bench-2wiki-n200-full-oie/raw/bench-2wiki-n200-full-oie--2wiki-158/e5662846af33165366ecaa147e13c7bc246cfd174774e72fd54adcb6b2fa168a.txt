Wrong Turn 2: Dead End
Wrong Turn 2: Dead End is a 2007 American horror film directed by Joe Lynch and starring Erica Leerhsen, Henry Rollins and Texas Battle. A sequel to" Wrong Turn"( 2003) and the second installment in the" Wrong Turn" franchise. It was released on DVD on October 9, 2007. The film received a positive response from critics making it the best- reviewed film in the franchise. It is followed by( 2009).
