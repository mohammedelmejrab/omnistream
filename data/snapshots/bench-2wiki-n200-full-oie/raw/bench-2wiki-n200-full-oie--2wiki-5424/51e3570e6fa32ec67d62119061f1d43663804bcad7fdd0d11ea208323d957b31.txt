Why Did It Have to Be Me?
" Why Did It Have to Be Me?" is a song by the Swedish pop band ABBA. The song was released on the 1976 album" Arrival Why Did It Have to Be Me?" has been called a" Fats Domino flavoured" song. The song has been noted for its mix of blues, jazz, pop and rock stylings. A live version of the song appears on the album" Live at Wembley Arena".
