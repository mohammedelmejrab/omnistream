Archibald Hamilton Rowan
Archibald Hamilton Rowan( 1 May 1751 – 1 November 1834), christened Archibald Hamilton( sometimes referred to as Archibald Rowan Hamilton), was a founding member of the Dublin Society of United Irishmen. He was the son of Gawen Hamilton( 1729 – 1805) of Killyleagh Castle, County Down and Lady Jane Rowan Hamilton.
