Beast of Blood (film)
Beast of Blood, released in the UK as Blood Devils, is a 1970 Filipino horror film. A sequel to" The Mad Doctor of Blood Island", it was directed by Eddie Romero. It was the fourth in a series of four Filipino horror films, produced by Romero and Kane W. Lynn, known as the" Blood Island" series, which also included" Terror Is a ManBrides of Blood" and" Beast of Blood". It was also Romero's last film for Lynn's Hemisphere Pictures, as the two went their separate ways after this film was completed.
