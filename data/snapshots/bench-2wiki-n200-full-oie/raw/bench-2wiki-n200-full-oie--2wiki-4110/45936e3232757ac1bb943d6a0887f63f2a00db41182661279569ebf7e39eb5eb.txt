Can't Forget You (Sonia song)
" Ca n't Forget You" was Sonia's second single, released in September 1989. The mid- tempo song later appeared on her debut album," Everybody Knows", released in 1990. Contrary to fan speculation," Ca n't Forget You" was written specifically for Sonia by Stock Aitken Waterman; it was not initially offered to Kylie Minogue.
