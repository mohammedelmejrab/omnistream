Johann Adolf I, Duke of Saxe-Weissenfels
Johann Adolf I, Duke of Saxe- Weissenfels( 2 November 1649, in Halle – 24 May 1697, in Weissenfels), was a duke of Saxe- Weissenfels- Querfurt and member of the House of Wettin. He was the first son of Augustus, Duke of Saxe- Weissenfels, and his first wife, Anna Maria of Mecklenburg- Schwerin.
