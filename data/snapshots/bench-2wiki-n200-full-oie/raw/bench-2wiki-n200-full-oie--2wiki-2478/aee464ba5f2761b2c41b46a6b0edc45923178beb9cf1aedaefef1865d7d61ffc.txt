Wolves of the Street
Wolves of the Street( also known as The Wolves of Wall Street or Wolves in Wall Street) is a 1920 American western silent film directed by Otis B. Thayer and starring Edmund Cobb and Vida Johnson. The film was shot in Steamboat Springs, Colorado by the Thayer's Art- O- Graf film company. Franklyn Farnum was originally cast for the lead role, but he did not appear in the completed film.
