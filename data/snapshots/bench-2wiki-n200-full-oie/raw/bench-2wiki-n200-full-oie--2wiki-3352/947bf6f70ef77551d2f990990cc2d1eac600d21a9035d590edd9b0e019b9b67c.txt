Deep Blue Sea 2
Deep Blue Sea 2 is a 2018 American science fiction horror film directed by Darin Scott. It is a stand- alone sequel to the 1999 film" Deep Blue Sea", and stars Danielle Savre, Michael Beach, and Rob Mayes. Released direct- to- video on April 17, 2018, a shark conservationist is hired to consult on a top- secret project on genetically- enhanced bull sharks funded by a pharmaceutical billionaire. However, the highly intelligent super- sharks turn on their masters and begin to pick them off one by one.
