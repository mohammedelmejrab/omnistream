Jan III of Oświęcim
Jan III of Oświęcim  (1366 – by 19 August 1405) was a Duke of Oświęcim since 1376 until his death. He was the eldest child and only son of Duke Jan II of Oświęcim by his wife Hedwig, daughter of Ludwik I the Fair, Duke of Brzeg. Initially, historians thought that Dukes Jan II and Jan III are the same person, until the discovery of further sources who confirmed Jan III's existence.
