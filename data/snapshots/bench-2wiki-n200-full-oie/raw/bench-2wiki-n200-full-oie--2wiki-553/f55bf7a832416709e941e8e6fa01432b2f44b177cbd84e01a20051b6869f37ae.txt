Wizards of the Lost Kingdom
Wizards of the Lost Kingdom is a 1985 Argentine- American sword and sorcery film written by Ed Naha and directed by Héctor Olivera. It stars Bo Svenson as Kor the Conqueror, Vidal Peterson as Simon, and Thom Christopher as Shurka. The film is an entry in a series of nine films that Roger Corman produced in Argentina during the 1980s; the first one being" Deathstalker".
