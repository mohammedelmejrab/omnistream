Bollywood Calling
Bollywood Calling is a 2001 Indian English-language dramedy film written and directed by Nagesh Kukunoor. The film starred Pat Cusick, Navin Nischol, Om Puri, and Perizaad Zorabian in pivotal roles. The film showcases the satirical nuances of bollywood. It was showcased at the Rome Film Festival, and the MAMI Film Festival.
