Tammy and the T-Rex
Tammy and the T-Rex is a 1994 science-fiction comedy film directed by Stewart Raffill and written by Raffill and Gary Brockette. The film, which stars Denise Richards, Paul Walker, Ellen Dubin, Terry Kiser, and Buck Flower, centers around a high school student named Tammy, whose boyfriend Michael has his brain implanted in the body of a robotic "Tyrannosaurus rex" by a mad scientist.
