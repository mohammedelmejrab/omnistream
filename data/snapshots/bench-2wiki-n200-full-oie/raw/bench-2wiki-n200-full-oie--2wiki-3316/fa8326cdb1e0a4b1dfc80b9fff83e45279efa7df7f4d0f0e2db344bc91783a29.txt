Bulldog Drummond at Bay (1947 film)
Bulldog Drummond at Bay is a 1947 American thriller film directed by Sidney Salkow and starring Ron Randell for the first time as the British sleuth and adventurer Bulldog Drummond. The cast also includes Anita Louise, Patrick O' Moore and Terry Kilburn. The film is loosely based on the novel" Bulldog Drummond at Bay" by H. C. McNeile.
