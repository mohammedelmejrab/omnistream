Frederick, Duke of Saxe-Weissenfels-Dahme
Frederick of Saxe- Weissenfels(" Frederick Erdmann"; b. Halle, 20 November 1673- d. Dahme, 16 April 1715), was a German prince member of the House of Wettin and Duke of Saxe- Weissenfels- Dahme. He was the sixth son of Augustus, Duke of Saxe- Weissenfels but first- born from his second marriage with Johanna Walpurgis of Leiningen- Westerburg.
