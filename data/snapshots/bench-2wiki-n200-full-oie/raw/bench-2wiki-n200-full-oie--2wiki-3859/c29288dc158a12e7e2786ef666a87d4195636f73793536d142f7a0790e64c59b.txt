Bruno Polius
Bruno Polius( 8 May 1958) or" Bruno Polius- Victoire" is a French singer. In the 1970s he was the lead- singer/ soloist of the boyband Les Poppys. Although the line- up of Les Poppys changed several times Bruno was the lead- singer on the bands largest hit" Non- Non Rien n'a changé". Bruno also recorded several records as solo- artist, mainly during the same years as his membership of Les Poppys( see below)
