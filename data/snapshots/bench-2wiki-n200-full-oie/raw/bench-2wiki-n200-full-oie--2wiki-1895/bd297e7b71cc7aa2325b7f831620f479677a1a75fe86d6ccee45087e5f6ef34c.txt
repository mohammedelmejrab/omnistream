Brian Else
Brian" Who" Else is a Canadian Studio/ Live Sound Engineer and record producer. He began his career in 1989 in Vancouver working out of studios such as Profile Studios, Little Mountain Sound Studios, and Greenhouse Studios amongst others. He makes his residence in Vancouver, Canada at his personal studio Crappy Jack. Brian also has done many tours all over the world as FOH sound and/ or road manager for such acts as DOA, Dayglo Abortions, Itch, Zimmers Hole, NoMeans No, and West of Hell. He is frequently credited as WHO ELSE or Brian' Who' Else.
