Hello, The Future!
Hello, The Future! is the name of a nerd-folk band. Nicole Dieker has been performing as Hello, The Future! since May, 2010, when she recorded and uploaded one song a week to YouTube for 100 consecutive weeks. The band has released several albums and often performs at pop culture conventions. Although usually introduced as a solo act ("My name is Nicole , my band is Hello, The Future! and I am the only person in my band"), Hello, The Future sometimes performs and/or records with other performers or even a full backing band; "Giant Robot Album" was a collaboration with the band The Long Holidays.
