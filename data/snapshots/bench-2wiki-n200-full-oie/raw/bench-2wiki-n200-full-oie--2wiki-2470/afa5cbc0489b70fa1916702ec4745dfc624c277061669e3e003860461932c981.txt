Henry of Limburg-Broich
Henry of Limburg- Broich(?- 1446) was the Count of Limburg- Broich from 1439- 1446. Henry was a son of Count Theodoric IV of Limburg- Styrum. When Count William I of Limburg- Styrum died in 1439, the County was partitioned with Henry receiving the Lordship of Broich. Henry died in 1449 and was succeeded by William.
