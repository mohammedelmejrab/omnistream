The Man on the Roof
The Man on the Roof is a 1976 Swedish police procedural- thriller film directed by Bo Widerberg. It is based on the novel" The Abominable Man" by Maj Sjöwall and Per Wahlöö. The film stars Carl- Gustaf Lindstedt, Sven Wollter, Thomas Hellberg and Håkan Serner. The plot follows a police officer Martin Beck and his colleagues trying to solve the murder of a senior policeman, known for his brutality against others. While the investigation is ongoing, a well- armed man climbs up on a roof in Stockholm. Widerberg was inspired by the 1971 American film" The French Connection", and Widerberg would make one more crime film in 1984:" The Man from Majorca". The film won two Guldbagge Awards in 1977, for Best Film and Best Actor( Håkan Serner).
