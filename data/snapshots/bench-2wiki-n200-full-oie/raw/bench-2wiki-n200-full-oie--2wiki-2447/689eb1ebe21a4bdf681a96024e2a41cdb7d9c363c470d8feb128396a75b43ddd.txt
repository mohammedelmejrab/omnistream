Atty Persse
Henry Seymour" Atty" Persse( 1869 – 1960) was a British racehorse trainer. He was Champion Trainer in 1930. Persse was commissioned a second- lieutenant in the Duke of Lancaster's Own Yeomanry on 28 June 1899, but resigned his commission on 13 September 1902. He co-wrote the novel" Trainer and Temptress" which formed the basis for the 1925 silent film of the same name.
