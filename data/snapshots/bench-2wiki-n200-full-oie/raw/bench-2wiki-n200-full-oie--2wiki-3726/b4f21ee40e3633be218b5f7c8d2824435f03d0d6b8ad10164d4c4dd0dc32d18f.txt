Universidad Autónoma de La Laguna
The Universidad Autónoma de La Laguna( Laguna Autonomous University or UAL) is an institute of higher education located in Torreón, Coahuila, Mexico. It was founded in December 1988. The university includes three schools: Secondary Education, Administrative Sciences and Sciences and Humanities, as well as a graduate office, night school, and international communication institute. UAL also operates a natural history museum and a university radio station, XHUAL -FM 98.7.
