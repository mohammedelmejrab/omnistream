Aren't You Kind Of Glad We Did?
"Aren't You Kinda Glad We Did? " is a song composed by George Gershwin, with lyrics by Ira Gershwin. Ira Gershwin added the lyrics in the mid-1940s, to an unused tune by his brother, George. It was introduced by Betty Grable and Dick Haymes in the 1947 film "The Shocking Miss Pilgrim".
