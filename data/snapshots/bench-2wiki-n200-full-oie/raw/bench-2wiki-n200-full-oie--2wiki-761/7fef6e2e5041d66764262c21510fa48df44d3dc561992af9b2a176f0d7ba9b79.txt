Mouna Geethangal
Mouna Geethangal is a 1981 Indian Tamil- language film, directed by K. Bhagyaraj. The film stars Bhagyaraj himself alongside Saritha. The film was a blockbuster and was remade into Hindi as" Ek Hi Bhool" in the same year. It was also remade in Telugu as" Sathyabhama", in Kannada as" Mane Devru", and in Malayalam as" Chanchattam"( 1991).
