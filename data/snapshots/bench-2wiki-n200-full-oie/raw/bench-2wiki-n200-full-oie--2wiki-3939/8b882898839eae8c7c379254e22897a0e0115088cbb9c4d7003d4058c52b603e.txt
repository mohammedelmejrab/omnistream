Into the Grizzly Maze
Into the Grizzly Maze (originally titled Red Machine, then changed to Endangered and later to Grizzly), is an American action horror-thriller film directed by David Hackl and written by J.R. Reher. When two estranged brothers reunite at their childhood home in the Alaskan wilderness, they set out on a two-day hike and are stalked by a massive, unrelenting, and bloodthirsty grizzly bear. The film stars James Marsden, Thomas Jane, Billy Bob Thornton, Piper Perabo and Scott Glenn. The film was released on video on demand on May 19, 2015, before a limited release on June 26, 2015.
