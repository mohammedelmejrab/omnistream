Goin' Coconuts
Goin' Coconuts is a 1978 American musical adventure comedy film directed by Howard Morris and starring Donny and Marie Osmond. The feature film was released in theaters by Columbia Pictures on October 18, 1978. It was both a critical and commercial failure. Shooting took place in Oahu, Hawaii from March 31 to May 1978, during the annual production hiatus for the Osmonds' popular variety TV series. It was Ted Cassidy's final film appearance before his death in January 1979( although he continued to amass a number of animation- voiceover credits).
