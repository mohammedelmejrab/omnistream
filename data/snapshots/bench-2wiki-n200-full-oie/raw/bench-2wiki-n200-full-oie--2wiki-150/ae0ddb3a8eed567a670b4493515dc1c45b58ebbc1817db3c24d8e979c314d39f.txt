Wrong Turn 5: Bloodlines
Wrong Turn 5: Bloodlines( also known as Wrong Turn 5) is a 2012 American horror film written and directed by Declan O'Brien. The film stars Camilla Arfwedson, Roxanne McKee and Doug Bradley. It is the fifth installment in the" Wrong Turn" franchise and a sequel to( 2011), leading the events of the 2003 film" Wrong Turn".
