Phalitamsha
Released in 1976 Phalitamsha is a romantic Kannada movie, directed by S. R. Puttanna Kanagal. It was written by Srinivasa Kulakarni under the Banner of A 1 Movietone. The movie paired newcomer Jai Jagadish with four heroines. Aarathi as expected has the meatiest role and delivers yet another power- packed performance. Despite a good- looking hero, top notch actresses and beautiful outdoor locales, Phalitamsha remains one of the few flops of ace director Puttana Kanagal's career.
