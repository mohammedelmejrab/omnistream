Munti Wayi
Munti Wayi( Ancash Quechua" munti" tree," wayi" house," tree house", Hispanicized spelling" Monte Huay") is a mountain in the Cordillera Blanca in the Andes of Peru. It is located in the Ancash Region, Bolognesi Province, Aquia District. It lies northeast of Rahu Kutaq and southeast of Pastu Ruri.
