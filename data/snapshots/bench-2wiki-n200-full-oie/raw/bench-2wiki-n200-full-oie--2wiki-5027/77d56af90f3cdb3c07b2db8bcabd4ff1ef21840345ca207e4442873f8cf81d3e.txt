Suhaagan
Suhaagan is a 1986 Indian drama film, produced by M. Arjuna Raju under the Roja Enterprises banner and directed by K. Raghavendra Rao. It stars Jeetendra, Sridevi, Padmini Kolhapure in the lead roles and music composed by Bappi Lahari. The film is a remake of the Tamil movie" Enkeyo Ketta Kural"( 1982), with Rajnikanth, Ambika, Radha, which was first remade as the Telugu movie" Bava Maradallu"( 1984), starring Sobhan Babu, Raadhika, Suhasini in the pivotal roles.
