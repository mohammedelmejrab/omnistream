Hands Up or I'll Shoot
Hands Up or I'll Shoot is an East German crime comedy film directed by Hans- Joachim Kasprzik, who wrote the script along with Rudi Strahl. Rolf Herricht starred as officer Holms. The picture was filmed in 1965, but in 1966 censors refused to allow it to be screened, and therefore post-production ceased. " Hands Up or I'll Shoot" was only completed and distributed in 2009, making it the last East German film to be released.
