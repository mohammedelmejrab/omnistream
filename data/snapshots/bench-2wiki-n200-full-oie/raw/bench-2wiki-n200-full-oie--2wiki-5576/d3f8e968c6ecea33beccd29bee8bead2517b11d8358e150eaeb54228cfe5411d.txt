Mungarina Minchu
Mungarina Minchu is a 1997 Indian Kannada language film directed by Rajendra Singh Babu starring Ramesh Arvind and Shilpa. The film is inspired from the 1942 Italian film" Quattro passi fra le nuvole Four Steps in the Clouds") and its 1995 American remake" A Walk in the Clouds". The music for the film was composed by V. Manohar. The film was considered as one of the best movies by Rajendra Singh Babu. The film was awarded the Best Feature Film in Kannada at the 45th National Film Awards.
