José Manuel Corral
José Manuel Corral( born October 28, 1968 in Santa Fe, Santa Fe, Argentina) is a lawyer, a political activist of the Unión Cívica Radical party, and a member of the Coalition Frente Progresista Cívico y Social of Santa Fe. He was the President of the Municipal Council of the City of Santa Fe de la Vera Cruz( 2010 – 2011). He has been reelected Mayor of the city of Santa Fe, Santa Fe province, Argentina,( 2011–2015/ 2015–2019).
