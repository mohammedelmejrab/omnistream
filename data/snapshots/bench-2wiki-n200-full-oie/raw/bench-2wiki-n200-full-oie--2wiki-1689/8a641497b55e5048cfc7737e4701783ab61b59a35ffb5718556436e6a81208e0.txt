Abdel Nasser Barakat
Abdel Nasser Barakat( born 15 May 1974) is a Palestinian football manager, who was an attacking midfielder for Al- Bireh until he retired. He was the head coach on an interim basis for the Palestine national football team for a single match in 2011. He took over the Palestine national football team in May 2015 after guiding the Olympic side for two years. He has coached several clubs in Palestine and all age groups of the Palestine national team setup. He was the manager of the Palestine national football team and a holder of a UEFA Pro certificate in coaching.
