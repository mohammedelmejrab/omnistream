Museum of Modern Glass Art, Eskişehir
Museum of Modern Glass Art is a museum dedicated to contemporary glass art in Odunpazarı, Eskişehir, Turkey. The museum was established with the cooperation of Eskişehir Metropolitan Municipality, Anadolu University and a private group for friends of glass in 2007. Turkey's first museum of its kind, it exhibits works of 58 domestic and 10 foreign artists.
