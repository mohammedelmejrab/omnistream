Fritz Lang
Friedrich Christian Anton" Fritz" Lang( December 5, 1890 – August 2, 1976) was an Austrian- German- American filmmaker, screenwriter, and occasional film producer and actor. One of the best- known" émigrés" from Germany's school of Expressionism, he was dubbed the" Master of Darkness" by the British Film Institute. Lang's most famous films include the groundbreaking futuristic" Metropolis"( 1927) and the also influential" M"( 1931), a film noir precursor that he made before he moved to the United States.
