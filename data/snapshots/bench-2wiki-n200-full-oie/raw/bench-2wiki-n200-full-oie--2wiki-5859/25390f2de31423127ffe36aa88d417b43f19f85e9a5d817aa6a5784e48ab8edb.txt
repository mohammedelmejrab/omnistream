Laura Silverman
Laura Jane Silverman( born June 10, 1966) is an American actress and the older sister of actress and comedian Sarah Silverman. She acted in and" The Sarah Silverman Program" portraying a fictionalized version of herself. She also stars as Jane Benson on" The Comeback" with Lisa Kudrow and voiced Laura, the sarcastic receptionist on the animated comedy television series" Dr. Katz, Professional Therapist".
