Brian Tse (writer)
Brian Tse  is the author of the Hong Kong children comic, "McMug and McDull" series. He is one of the two creators (another is Alice Mak, his colleague-turned-wife) of the cartoon characters McMug and McDull. His wife is responsible for the illustration. His other works include The Excreman (a "serious" parody of the famous English cartoon, The Snowman), The Beautiful Loser, Pig Has Come, and Over the Rainbow.
