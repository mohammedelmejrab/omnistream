Mr. Ace
Mr. Ace is a 1946 American film noir drama film about a society woman( Sylvia Sidney) who taps a gangster( George Raft) for his political support as she runs for Congress. The movie was written by Fred F. Finkelhoffe and directed by Edwin L. Marin. George Raft later appeared in a radio show" The Cases of Mr. Ace". Mr Ace was turned into a private detective.
