You've Got a Good Love Comin' (song)
" You've Got a Good Love Comin'" is a song written by Van Stephenson, Jeff Silbar and Danny Morrison, and recorded by Van Stephenson on his 1981 album" China Girl". It was later released in December 1984 by American country music artist Lee Greenwood as the third single and title track from his album" You've Got a Good Love Comin'". Greenwood's version reached# 9 on the" Billboard" Hot Country Singles& Tracks chart.
