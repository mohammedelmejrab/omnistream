Madhavrao II
Peshwa Madhav Rao II (18 April 1774 – 27 October 1795) ( a.k.a. Sawai Madhavrao Peshwa or Madhav Rao II Narayan) was the 11th Peshwa of the Maratha Empire in India, from his infancy. He was known as Sawai Madhav Rao or Madhav Rao Narayan. He was the posthumous son of Narayanrao Peshwa, murdered in 1773 on the orders of Raghunathrao. Madhavrao II was considered the legal heir, and was installed as "Peshwa" by the Treaty of Salbai in 1782.
