Charles of Mecklenburg-Güstrow
Charles, Duke of Mecklenburg- Güstrow( 18 November 1664 in Güstrow – 15 March 1688 in Güstrow) was the hereditary prince of Mecklenburg- Güstrow. He was a son of Gustavus Adolph and his wife Magdalene Sibylle née Duchess of Schleswig- Holstein- Gottorp, a daughter of Frederick III.
