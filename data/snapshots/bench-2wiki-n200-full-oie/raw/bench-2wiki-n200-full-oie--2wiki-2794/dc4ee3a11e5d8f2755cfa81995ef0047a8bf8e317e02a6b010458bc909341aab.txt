Walt Gorney
Walter J. Gorney( April 12, 1912 – March 5, 2004) was an Austrian- born American actor. He was best known for his role as" Crazy" Ralph in the 1980s slasher films" Friday the 13th"( 1980) and" Friday the 13th Part 2"( 1981). He returned to the series in( 1988) as the narrator. His other film credits include" Heavy Traffic"( 1981)," King Kong"( 1976), and" Day of the Animals"( 1977). Walter's character Ralph was parodied by" Robert Donner" in the 1983 film" Hysterical". On March 5, 2004, Gorney died in New York City, aged 91.
