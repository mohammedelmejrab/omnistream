Jyrki Nurminen
Jyrki Nurminen( born 2 November 1990 in Ristijärvi, Finland) is a Finnish beach volleyball player, playing as a defender. He won the Finnish beach volleyball championship 4 times, in 2012, 2016, 2017 and in 2018. During his career, Nurminen also played volleyball in Finnish championship league, but since 2016, he focuses entirely on the beach volleyball career. His partner in beach volleyball is currently Santeri Sirén. Nurminen has received the first level coaching volleyball license and works also as a coach of both, beach volleyball and volleyball. He is a founder of a company arranging beach volleyball events and camps.
