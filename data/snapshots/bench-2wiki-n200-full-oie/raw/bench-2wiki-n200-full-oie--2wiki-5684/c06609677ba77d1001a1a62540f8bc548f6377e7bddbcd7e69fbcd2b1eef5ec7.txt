Ned Rifle
Ned Rifle is a 2014 American drama film written and directed by Hal Hartley. It is the third and final film in a trilogy following characters introduced in Hartley's 1997 film" Henry Fool" and 2006 sequel" Fay GrimNed Rifle" stars Liam Aiken as the title character, reprising his role from the other two films, as well as Aubrey Plaza, Parker Posey, James Urbaniak, and Thomas Jay Ryan. The film premiered on September 7, 2014 at the 2014 Toronto International Film Festival. The film was released in select theaters and on demand beginning on April 1, 2015.
