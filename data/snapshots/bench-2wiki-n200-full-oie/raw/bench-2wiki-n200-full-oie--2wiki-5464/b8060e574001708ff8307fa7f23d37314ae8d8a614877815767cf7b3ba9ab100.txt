Nude on the Moon
Nude on the Moon is a 1961 science- fantasy nudist film co-written and co-directed by Doris Wishman and Raymond Phelan under the shared pseudonyms" O. O. Miller" and" Anthony Brooks". The film was produced in 1960 and theatrically released in 1961. " Nude on the Moon" is the second of eight nudist films produced and directed by Wishman. While most nudist films of the time were set within a conventional nudist camp, apparently as a promotional gimmick Wishman decided to set the story in a nudist civilization on the Moon.
