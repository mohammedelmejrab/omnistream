Michel'le
Michel'le Denise Toussant( born December 5, 1970), known mononymously as Michel'le, is an American R&B singer and songwriter. She is best known for her songs from the late 1980s to early 1990s. Her most notable songs were two" Billboard" R&B chart toppers" No More Lies"( a top 10 hit on the Hot 100) and" Something in My Heart".
