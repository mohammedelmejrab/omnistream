Prince Georg Philipp of Saxe-Gessaphe
Georg Philipp, Prince of Saxe- Gessaphe( born 24 May 1988 in Mexico City, Mexico), is the eldest son and heir of Alexander, Prince of Saxe- Gessaphe and Princess Gisela of Bavaria. He is a second cousin of Archduke Sigismund, Grand Duke of Tuscany and Henri Antoine, Hereditary Prince of Ligne. He is also third cousin of Sophie, Hereditary Princess of Liechtenstein. He is a fifth cousin of Duke Friedrich of Württemberg. He is a 6th cousin of Prince Amedeo of Belgium, Archduke of Austria- Este and Ferdinand Zvonimir von Habsburg. He stands to become pretender to the Saxon throne after his father.
