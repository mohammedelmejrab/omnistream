Mere Rashke Qamar
"Mere Rashke Qamar" (Hindi: "मेरे रश्के क़मर"; lit. " O Envy of the Moon") is a ghazal-qawwali written by Urdu poet Fana Buland Shehri and composed by Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan. It was first performed in 1988 by Khan, and popularized by him and his nephew Rahat Fateh Ali Khan several times in different concerts.
