Bolesław III the Generous
Boleslaw III the Wasteful( 23 September 1291 – Brieg, 21 April 1352), was a Duke of Legnica, Brzeg( Brieg) from 1296 until 1342, and Duke of Wrocław from 1296 until 1311. He was the eldest son of Henry V the Fat, Duke of Legnica and Wrocław, by his wife Elisabeth, daughter of Bolesław the Pious, Duke of Greater Poland.
