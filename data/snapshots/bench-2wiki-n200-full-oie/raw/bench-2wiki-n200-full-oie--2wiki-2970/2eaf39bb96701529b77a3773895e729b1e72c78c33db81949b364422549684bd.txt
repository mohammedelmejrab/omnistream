Louis, Count of Évreux
Louis of Évreux( 3 May 1276 – 19 May 1319, Paris) was a prince, the only son of King Philip III of France and his second wife Maria of Brabant, and thus a half- brother of King Philip IV of France. Louis had a quiet and reflective personality and was politically opposed to the scheming of his half- brother Charles of Valois. He was, however, close with his nephew Philip V of France.
