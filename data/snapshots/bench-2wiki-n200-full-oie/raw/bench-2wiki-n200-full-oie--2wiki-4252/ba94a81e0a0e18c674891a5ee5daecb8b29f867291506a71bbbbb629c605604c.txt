Henry Howard, 4th Earl of Effingham
Henry Alexander Gordon Howard, 4th Earl of Effingham DL( 15 August 1866 – 6 May 1927) was an English peer and member of the House of Lords, styled Lord Howard from 1889 to 1898. He inherited the earldom from his father, Henry Howard, 3rd Earl of Effingham in 1898. Effingham was a Deputy Lieutenant of the West Riding of Yorkshire. He was a Liberal Unionist in politics. He was succeeded in the earldom by his first cousin, Gordon Howard, 5th Earl of Effingham.
