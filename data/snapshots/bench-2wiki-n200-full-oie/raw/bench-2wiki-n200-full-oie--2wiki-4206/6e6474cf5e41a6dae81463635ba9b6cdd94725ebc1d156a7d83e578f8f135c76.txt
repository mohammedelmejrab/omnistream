A Chrysanthemum Bursts in Cincoesquinas
Un crisantemo estalla en cinco esquinas  is a 1998 Argentine, Brazilian, French, and Spanish comedy drama film written and directed by Daniel Burman, in feature film debut. It was produced by Diego Dubcovsky. It stars José Luis Alfonzo, Pastora Vega and Martin Kalwill, among others. Film critic Anthony Kaufman, writing for "indieWIRE", an online community of independent filmmakers and aficionados, said Burman's "A Chrysanthemum Burst in Cincoesquinas" (1998) has been cited as the beginning of the "New Argentine Cinema" wave.
