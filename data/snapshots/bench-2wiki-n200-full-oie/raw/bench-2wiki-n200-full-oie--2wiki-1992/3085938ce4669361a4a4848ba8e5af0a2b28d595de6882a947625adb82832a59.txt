3096 Days
3096 Days is a 2013 German drama film directed by Sherry Hormann. The film is based on the true story of Natascha Kampusch, a 10- year- old girl and her eight- year ordeal being kidnapped by Wolfgang Přiklopil. Northern Irish actress Antonia Campbell- Hughes portrays Kampusch, while Thure Lindhardt plays Přiklopil. The film was writer Bernd Eichinger's last film before his sudden death, and Martin Moskowicz, head of TV and Film at Constantin Film, and Ruth Toma(" Gloomy Sunday") took over.
