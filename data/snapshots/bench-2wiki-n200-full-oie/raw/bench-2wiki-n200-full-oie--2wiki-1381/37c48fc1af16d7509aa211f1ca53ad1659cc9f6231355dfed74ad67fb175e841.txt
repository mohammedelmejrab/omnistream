Sidhi (film)
Sidhi is a 1999 Filipino film based on Rolando Tinio's Palanca- winning teleplay(" Ang Kwento ni Ah") expanded into a movie script by Ricky Lee," Sidhi" is the story of Ah( Aunor), so called because she's mute and can only utter the first syllable of her name Anna. The film won for Mercado all the Best Supporting Actress Awards given by the four annual award- giving bodies in the Philippines that time. The film also gave a posthumous award to Rolando Tinio for Best story at the FAMAS Awards.
