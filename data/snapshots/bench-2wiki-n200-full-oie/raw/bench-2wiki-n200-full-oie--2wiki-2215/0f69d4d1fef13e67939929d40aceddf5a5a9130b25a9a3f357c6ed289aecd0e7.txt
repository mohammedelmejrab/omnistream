Colin Maitland
Colin Maitland( born 12 August 1942) is an English actor who has made several film and television appearances. He is notable for portraying Seth Sawyer, a member of" The Dirty Dozen" in the 1967 film of that name. He is married to Amanda who he met on a blind date He appeared on BBC's Bargain Hunt 3 November 2017. He also appeared in" Lolita( 1962)" and" The Bedford Incident"( 1965). His television roles include the sound/ studio engineer in" Shoestring"( 1979 – 80). In 2006 he recounted his experiences on" The Dirty Dozen" in a ‘ making of ’ documentary.
