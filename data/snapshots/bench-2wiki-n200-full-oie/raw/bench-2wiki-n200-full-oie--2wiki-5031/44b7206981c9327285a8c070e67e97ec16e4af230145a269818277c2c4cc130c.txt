Midida Hrudayagalu
Midida Hrudayagalu is a 1993 Indian Kannada romantic drama film, directed by A. T. Raghu and written by Shantharaj. The film features Ambareesh, Shruti and Nirosha in the lead roles. The film is regarded as one of more popular movies of Shruti. The film is a remake of the 1982 Tamil movie" Enkeyo Ketta Kural".
