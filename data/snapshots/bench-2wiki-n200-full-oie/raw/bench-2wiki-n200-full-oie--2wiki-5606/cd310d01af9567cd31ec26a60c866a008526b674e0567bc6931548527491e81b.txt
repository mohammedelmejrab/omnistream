Giovanni dei Conti di Segni
Giovanni dei Conti di Segni( died June 14, 1213) was an Italian cardinal and cardinal- nephew of Pope Innocent III, his cousin, who elevated him in 1200 with the deaconry of Santa Maria in Cosmedin. He was also Chancellor of the Holy Roman Church 1205 – 1213 and Protodeacon of the Sacred College from 1210 or 1211.
