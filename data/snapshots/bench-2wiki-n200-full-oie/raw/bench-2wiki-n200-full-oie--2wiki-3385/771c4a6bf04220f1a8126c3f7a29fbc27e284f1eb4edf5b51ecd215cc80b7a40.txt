Robert Hovenden
Robert Hovenden D.D.( 1544–1614) was an English academic administrator at the University of Oxford. Hovenden was elected Warden( head) of All Souls College, Oxford in 1571, a post he held until 1614. During his time as Warden of All Souls College, he was also Vice- Chancellorof Oxford University during 1582 – 3. Hovenden was a humanist, undertook building work for All Souls College, and produced strip maps of the College estates.
