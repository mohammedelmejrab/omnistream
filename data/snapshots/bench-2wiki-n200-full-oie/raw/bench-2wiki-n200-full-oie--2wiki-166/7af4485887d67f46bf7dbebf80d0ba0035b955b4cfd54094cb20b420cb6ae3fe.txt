You Can No Longer Remain Silent
You Can No Longer Remain Silent( German: Du darfst nicht länger schweigen) is a 1955 West German romantic drama film directed by Robert A. Stemmle and starring Heidemarie Hatheyer, Wilhelm Borchert and Werner Hinz. It is based on the 1929 novel" Morning of Life" by Kristmann Gudmundsson. It is set amongst feuding Scandinavian fishing families. The film's sets were designed by the art directors Helmut Nentwig and Karl Weber.
