Pratisodh (2004 film)
Pratisodh is a 2004 Bengali film directed by Anup Sengupta and produced by Shela Kalyani. The film features actors Prosenjit Chatterjee, Rachana Banerjee, Tapas Paul, Deepankar De, Dulal Lahiri, Anamika Saha in the lead roles. Music of the film has been composed by Ashok Bhadra.
