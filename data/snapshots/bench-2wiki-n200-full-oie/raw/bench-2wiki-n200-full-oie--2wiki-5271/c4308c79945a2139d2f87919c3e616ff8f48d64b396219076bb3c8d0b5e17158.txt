Marie-Lambertine Coclers
Marie- Lambertine Coclers( 1761 – after 1815) was a Southern Netherlandish pastellist and engraver. Born in Liège, Coclers was the daughter of Jean- Baptiste Coclers, who died during her childhood. Her instructor was her elder brother, Jean- Baptiste- Bernard Coclers; he painted an image of a young artist which might depict his sister.
