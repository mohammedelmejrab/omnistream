Pellaina Kothalo
Pellaina Kothalo (English: "While Just Married") is a 2006 Telugu film produced & directed by Madan on Aa Naluguru Films banner and Cinematography by T. Surendra Reddy. Starring Jagapati Babu, Priyamani in lead roles and music composed by Agashtya. The film recorded as "Super Hit" at box office.
