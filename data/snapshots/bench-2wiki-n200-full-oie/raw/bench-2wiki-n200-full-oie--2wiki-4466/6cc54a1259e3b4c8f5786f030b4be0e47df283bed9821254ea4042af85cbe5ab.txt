Man on Fire (1987 film)
Man on Fire is a 1987 action thriller film directed by Élie Chouraqui and starring Scott Glenn and Jade Malle. It is based on the 1980 novel" Man on Fire" by A. J. Quinnell. The rest of the cast includes Joe Pesci, Jonathan Pryce and a brief appearance by Danny Aiello as the Mafia don. Another film based on the same novel, also called" Man on Fire", was released in 2004, directed by Tony Scott.
