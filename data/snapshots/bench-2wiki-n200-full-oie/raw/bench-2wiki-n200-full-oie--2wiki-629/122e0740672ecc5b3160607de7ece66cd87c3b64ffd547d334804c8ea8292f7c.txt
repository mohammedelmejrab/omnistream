Bruce Leung
Bruce Leung Siu-lung( born Leung Choi-sang; April 28, 1948) is an actor who has appeared in many Hong Kong martial arts movies. He often appeared billed as" Bruce LeungBruce LiangBruce Leong", or" Bruce Leung Siu-lung", and is thus generally grouped among the Bruce Lee clones that sprang up after Lee's death in the subgenre known as Bruceploitation.
