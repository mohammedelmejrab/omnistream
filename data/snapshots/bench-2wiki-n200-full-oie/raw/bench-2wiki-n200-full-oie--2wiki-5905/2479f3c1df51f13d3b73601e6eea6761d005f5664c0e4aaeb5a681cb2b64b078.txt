Hans Bollandsås
Hans Bollands ås also known as just Hans( born in Melhus, Sør- Trøndelag, Norway on October 28, 1980) is a Norwegian blues musician who won the Norwegian X Factor in 2010. In a final that was held on 11 December 2010, by beating Atle Pettersen and Annsofi Pettersen, to the top. He was mentored by judge Elisabeth Andreassen in the over 25 category. Earlier in 2010, he had won the Notodden national competition in blues guitar. He is married and has two children.
