USS Hamilton (DD-141)
The second USS" Hamilton"( DD – 141) was a in the United States Navy following World War I, later reclassified DMS- 18 for service in World War II. She was the first ship named for Lieutenant Archibald Hamilton. " Hamilton" was launched on 15 January 1919 by the Mare Island Naval Shipyard, sponsored by Miss Dolly Hamilton Hawkins, great- grand- niece of Archibald Hamilton. The ship was commissioned on 7 November 1919, Lieutenant Commander R. G. Coman in command.
