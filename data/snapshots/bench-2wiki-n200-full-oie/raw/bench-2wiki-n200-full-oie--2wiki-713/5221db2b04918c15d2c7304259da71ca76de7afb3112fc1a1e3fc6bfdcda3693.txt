Alexander Singer
Alexander Singer( born 18 April 1928, in New York City, New York) is an American director. He began his career behind the camera in 1951 as a cinematographer on the short documentary" Day of the Fight", directed by his high school friend, Stanley Kubrick. Singer himself turned to directing a decade later with the film," A Cold Wind in August". Although he would direct other feature films, such as the Lee Van Cleef western," Captain Apache"( 1971), and" Glass Houses"( 1972), a film of the book which his wife, Judith Singer, wrote, the bulk of Singer's credits are in television.
