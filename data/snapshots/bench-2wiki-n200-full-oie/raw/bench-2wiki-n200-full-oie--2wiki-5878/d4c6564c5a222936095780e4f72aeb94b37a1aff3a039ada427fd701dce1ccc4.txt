Wilhelmine Schröder-Devrient
Wilhelmine Schröder- Devrient, born Wilhelmine Schröder( 6 December 180426 January 1860), was a German operatic soprano. As a singer, she combined a rare quality of tone with dramatic intensity of expression, which was as remarkable on the concert platform as in opera. Today, she is remembered as the author of the most celebrated erotic novel in the German language.
