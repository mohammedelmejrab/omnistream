Johann Adolf II, Duke of Saxe-Weissenfels
Johann Adolf II, Duke of Saxe- Weissenfels ( Weissenfels, 4 September 1685 – Leipzig, 16 May 1746), was the last duke of Saxe- Weissenfels- Querfurt and a member of the House of Wettin. He was also a commander in the Saxon army. Johann Adolf was the youngest of the eleven children of Johann Adolf I, Duke of Saxe- Weissenfels, and Johanna Magdalena of Saxe- Altenburg. His mother died five months after his birth, on 22 January 1686.
