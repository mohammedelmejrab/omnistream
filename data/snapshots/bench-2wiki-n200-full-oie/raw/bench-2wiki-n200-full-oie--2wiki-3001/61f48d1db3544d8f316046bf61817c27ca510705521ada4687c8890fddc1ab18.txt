Aram + Aram = Kinnaram
Aram+ Aram= Kinnaram is a 1985 Indian Malayalam- language slapstick comedy film directed by Priyadarshan and written by Sreenivasan. It stars Shankar, Mohanlal, Lissy and Puja Saxena. The plot element is inspired by 2 Hindi films," Pasand Apni Apni" and" Naram Garam". A famous hotel comedy sequence from this movie was reused by Priyadarshan in his 2005 Hindi movie" Garam Masala".
