Seven Women (1953 film)
Seven Women( Spanish: Siete Mujeres) is a 1953 Mexican drama film directed by Juan Bustillo Oro and starring Amelia Bence, Alma Rosa Aguirre and Bárbara Gil. It is based on a play of the same name which had previously been adapted into the 1944 Argentine film" Seven Women". The film's sets were designed by the art director Manuel Fontanals.
