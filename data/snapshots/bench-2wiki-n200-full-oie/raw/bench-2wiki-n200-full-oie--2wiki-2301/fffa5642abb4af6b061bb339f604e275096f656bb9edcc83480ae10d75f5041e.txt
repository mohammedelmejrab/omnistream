Cristina Kahlo
Cristina Kahlo y Calderón( 1908- 1964) was the sister of artist Frida Kahlo. Frida painted a portrait of Cristina, titled" Portrait of Cristina, My Sister", and Diego Rivera, Frida's husband, also portrayed Cristina Kahlo in his work. Rivera painted Cristina in the nude, which hint towards the pair's affair.
