3 Dots
3 Dots is a 2013 Malayalam comedy film directed by Sugeeth. Kunchacko Boban and Biju Menon in lead roles with Prathap K. Pothan, Narain and Krishnakumar in supporting roles. Janani Iyer plays the female lead. Anjana Menon and Sreedhanya( Gayathri) plays supporting roles." 3 Dots" is being produced by Satish B Satish and Sugeeth under the banner of Ordinary Films.
