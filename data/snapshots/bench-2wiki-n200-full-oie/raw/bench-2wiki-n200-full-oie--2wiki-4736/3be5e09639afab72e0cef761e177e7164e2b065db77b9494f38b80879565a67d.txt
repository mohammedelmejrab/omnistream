Rijk de Gooyer
Rijk de Gooyer( 17 December 1925 – 2 November 2011) was a Dutch Golden Calf- winning actor, writer, comedian and singer. From the 1950s until the early 1970s, he became well known in The Netherlands as part of a comic duo with John Kraaijkamp, Sr. In the United States best known for having small roles in films such as," Soldaat van OranjeA Time to Die" and" The Wilby Conspiracy".
