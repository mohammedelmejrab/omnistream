Lawson Harris
William Lawson Harris( June 30, 1897 – March 31, 1948) was an American director, actor, producer, and writer who made a number of films in Australia. He came to Australia originally at the behest of Arthur Shirley to work as production manager on" The Throwback"( 1920) and stayed out there for a number of years. Harris was the father of actor and director John Derek.
