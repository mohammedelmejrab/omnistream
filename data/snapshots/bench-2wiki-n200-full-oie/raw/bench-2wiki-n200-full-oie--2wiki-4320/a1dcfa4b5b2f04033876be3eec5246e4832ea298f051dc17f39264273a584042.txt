Apeejay Education Society
Apeejay Education Society( AES), established in India in 1967 by late Dr. Stya Paul, is a private educational society. It consists of 14 Schools and 13 Institutions of higher learning imparting education in India. Currently Sushma Berlia is running this organization. All the Apeejay schools are members of the progressive Schools Conference( NPSC) and are affiliated to the Central Board of Secondary Education( CBSE). It has been accredited to the British Council for London Examination Qualifications( for both GCE' O'&' A' level courses). In 2010 Apeejay education society started Apeejay Stya University in collaboration with the Apeejay Education Society in Gurgaon, Haryana.
