Lina Wertmüller
Lina Wertmüller( born 14 August 1928) is an Italian screenwriter and film director. She was the first woman nominated for an Academy Award for Best Director for" Seven Beauties" in 1977. She is also known for her films" The Seduction of MimiLove and Anarchy" and" Swept Away". In 2019, Wertmüller was announced as one of the four recipients of the Academy Honorary Award for her career.
