Anna of Brunswick-Grubenhagen-Einbeck
Anna of Brunswick- Grubenhagen- Einbeck( 1414 – 4 April 1474) was a daughter of Duke Eric I of Brunswick- Grubenhagen and his wife, Elisabeth of Brunswick- Göttingen. Anna's first marriage was with Duke Albert III of Bavaria. They had the following children: After Albert's death, she married Duke Frederick III of Brunswick- Calenberg- Göttingen. This marriage remained childless.
