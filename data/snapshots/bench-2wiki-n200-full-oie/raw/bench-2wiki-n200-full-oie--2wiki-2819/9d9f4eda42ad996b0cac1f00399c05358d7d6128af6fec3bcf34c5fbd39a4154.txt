Azimullah Khan
Azimullah Khan Yusufzai( 1830- 1859), also known as" Dewan Azimullah Khan", was initially appointed Secretary, and later Prime Minister( hence the prefix" Dewan") to Nana Sahib. He is also known as the" Krantidoot Azimullah KhanKrantidoot" in Hindi for" Ambassador of Revolution"). Azimullah Khan was involved in the Indian Rebellion of 1857, primarily ideologically, influencing important nobles such as Nana Sahib.
