Augusta Marie of Holstein-Gottorp
Augusta Marie of Holstein- Gottorp( 1649 – 1728) was a German noblewoman. She was the daughter of Frederick III, Duke of Holstein- Gottorp and Duchess Marie Elisabeth of Saxony. Through her daughter Albertine Frederica, she is the great- grandmother of Catherine II and great- great- grandmother of Paul I of Russia.
