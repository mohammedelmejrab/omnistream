Chaowa Pawa (2009 film)
Chaowa Pawa is a 2009 Bengali film directed by Swapan Saha. The film is starring Prasenjit, Rachana Banerjee, Swarna Kamal Dutta, Santana Bose, Bodhisattwa Majumdar, Abhishek Chatterjee, Ramen Roy Chowdhury, Locket Chatterjee.
