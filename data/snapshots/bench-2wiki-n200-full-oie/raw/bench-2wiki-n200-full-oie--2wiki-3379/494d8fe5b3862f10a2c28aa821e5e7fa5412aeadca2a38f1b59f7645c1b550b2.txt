Tatiana Schlossberg
Tatiana Celia Kennedy Schlossberg (born May 5, 1990) is an American journalist and author. She is the daughter of Caroline Kennedy, the former U.S. Ambassador to Japan, and a granddaughter of John F. Kennedy, the 35th U.S. president. A reporter for "The New York Times" covering climate change, she has also written for "The Atlantic". Her book is "Inconspicuous Consumption" (2019).
