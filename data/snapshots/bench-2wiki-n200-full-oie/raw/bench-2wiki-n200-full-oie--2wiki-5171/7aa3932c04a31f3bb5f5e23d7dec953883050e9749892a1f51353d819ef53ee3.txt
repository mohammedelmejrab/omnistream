A Tale of Winter
A Tale of Winter  is a 1992 French drama film directed by Éric Rohmer, and starring Charlotte Véry, Frédéric van den Driessche and Michael Voletti. It is the second of Rohmer's "Tales of the Four SeasonsContes des quatre saisons"), which also include "A Tale of Springtime" (1990), "A Summer's Tale" (1996) and "Autumn Tale" (1998). The film was entered into the 42nd Berlin International Film Festival.
