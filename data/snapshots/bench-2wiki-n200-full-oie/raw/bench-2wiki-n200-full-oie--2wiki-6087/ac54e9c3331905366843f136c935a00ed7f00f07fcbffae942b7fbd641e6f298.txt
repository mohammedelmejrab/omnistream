Archambaud VIII of Bourbon
Archambaud VIII of Bourbon, nicknamed "the Great", (1189–1242) was a ruler ("sire") of Bourbonnais in the modern region of Auvergne, France. His parents were Guy II of Dampierre and Mathilde of Bourbon. Archambaud’s first wife was Alix de Forez. They married in 1205.
