Eman Lam (dancer)
Eman Lam( Chinese name: 林伊汶) is a Hong Kong dancer, singer, comedian and painter. She is the founder and artistic director of Le Jeu dance and Stand Up HK comedy companies. In 2016, she started a stand- up comedy platform called Stand Up HK 香港是但噏- the only English and Cantonese stand up company in Hong Kong for local comedians.
