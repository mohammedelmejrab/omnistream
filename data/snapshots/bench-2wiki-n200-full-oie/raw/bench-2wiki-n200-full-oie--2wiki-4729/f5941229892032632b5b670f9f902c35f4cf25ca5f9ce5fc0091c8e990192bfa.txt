Meri Jung
Meri Jung is a 1985 Indian Hindi movie produced by N N Sippy and directed by Subhash Ghai. The film stars Anil Kapoor, Meenakshi Sheshadri, Nutan, Amrish Puri, Javed Jaffrey( in his debut film), A. K. Hangal, Iftekhar, Kushboo and Parikshit Sahni. The film's music is by Laxmikant Pyarelal and lyrics wrote by Anand Bakshi. The film was a huge box office success and was critically acclaimed. It was a breakthrough performance for Anil Kapoor and put him into the big league. Kapoor's performance in the film is considered to be one of his best and he received his first Filmfare Award nomination; under the Best Actor Category. The film was also nominated under the Best Film category.
