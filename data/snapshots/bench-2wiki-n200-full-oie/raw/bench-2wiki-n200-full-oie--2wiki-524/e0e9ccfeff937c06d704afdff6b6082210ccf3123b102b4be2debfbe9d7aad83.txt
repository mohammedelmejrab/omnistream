Christopher of Baden-Durlach
Christopher of Baden- Durlach( 9 October 1684, Karlsburg Castle, Durlach – 2 May 1723, Karlsruhe) was Prince and( titular) Margrave of Baden- Durlach. Christopher was the son of Margrave Frederick VII Magnus of Baden- Durlach and Augusta Marie of Holstein- Gottorp( born February 6, 1649 – † April 25, 1728), daughter of the Duke Frederick III of Holstein- Gottorp. He was the brother of the ruling Margrave Charles III William, who reigned from 1709 till 1738.
