William Feilding, 11th Earl of Denbigh
William Rudolph Michael Feilding, 11th Earl of Denbigh( 1943 – 1995)( Peerage of England, cr 1622), and 10th Earl of Desmond( Peerage of Ireland, also cr 1622) died on 23 March 1995. He married Caroline Judith Vivienne Cooke on 2 September 1965. They had three children: Lady Samantha, Lady Louisa, and Alexander, the current 12th Earl of Denbigh and 11th Earl of Desmond.
