Henry Lennard, 12th Baron Dacre
Henry Lennard, 12th Baron Dacre (25 March 1570 – 8 August 1616) was an English baron and politician. He was the son of Margaret Fiennes, 11th Baroness Dacre and Sampson Lennard.
