The Editor (film)
The Editor is a 2014 Canadian black comedy giallo film by Astron- 6 and starring Paz de la Huerta, Adam Brooks, Laurence R. Harvey, and Udo Kier. The film is an homage to and parody of" giallo", a cinematic and literary subgenre originating in Italy and popularized through movies like" The Bird with the Crystal Plumage, Torso, Deep Red," and" A Lizard in a Woman's Skin". The film was shot on- location in Winnipeg, Manitoba and Kenora, Ontario, and premiered at the 2014 Toronto International Film Festival. It was released on home video and VOD platforms on September 8, 2015, and received mixed- to- positive reviews.
