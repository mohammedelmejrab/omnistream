Days and Hours
Days and Hours (Bosnian version title: "Kod amidže Idriza") is a 2004 Bosnian film directed by Pjer Žalica and written by Namik Kabil. It was Bosnia and Herzegovina's submission to the 77th Academy Awards for the Academy Award for Best Foreign Language Film, but it was not nominated. The film was released on 20 August 2004.
