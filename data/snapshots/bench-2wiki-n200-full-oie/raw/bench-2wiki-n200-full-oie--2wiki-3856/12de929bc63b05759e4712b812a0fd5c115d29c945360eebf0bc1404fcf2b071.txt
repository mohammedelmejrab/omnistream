Eko.Lsa
Eko. Lsa, Eko, Eko. Bacchus, or Bruno is a French hip hop performer. A native of Réunion island in Indian Ocean, he later moved to Toulouse for studies and then Pau, Pyrénées- Atlantiques, in metropolitan France. He released his first album," Cinémascope", in 2004 and one of its songs," An de Grâce 2003", was featured on the premiere episode of the American television series" Kidnapped". He was a well- known graffiti painter from the biggest crew L.S.A. in the early 1990s. Then, in 1996, he made a first single," Savate et caleçon", which was a hit in the nightclubs of the island.
