Jan IV of Oświęcim
Jan IV of Oświęcim( 1426/1430 – by 21 February 1497), was a Duke of Oświęcim during 1434 – 1456( until 1445 with his brothers as co-rulers) and Duke of Gliwice from 1465 to 1482. He was the third son of Duke Casimir I of Oświęcim by his first wife Anna, daughter of Duke Henry VIII of Żagań.
