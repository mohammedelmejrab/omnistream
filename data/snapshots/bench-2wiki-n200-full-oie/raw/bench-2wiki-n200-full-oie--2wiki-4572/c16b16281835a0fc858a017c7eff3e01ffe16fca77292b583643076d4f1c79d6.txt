KBFL (AM)
KBFL( 1060 AM) is a radio station broadcasting a Music Of Your Life format. Formerly, the station was smooth jazz. Until September 1, 2013, KBFL broadcast the Music of Your Life adult standards format, simulcasting its sister station, KBFL- FM. The station is licensed to Springfield, Missouri and is owned by Meyer- Baldridge, Inc.( d/ b/a Meyer Communications). KBFL returned to Music of Your Life on March 2, 2015.
