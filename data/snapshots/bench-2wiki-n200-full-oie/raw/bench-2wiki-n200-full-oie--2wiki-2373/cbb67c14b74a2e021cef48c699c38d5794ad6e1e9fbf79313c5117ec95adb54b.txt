Jan Löwe
Jan Löwe( born 14 July 1967) is a German molecular and structural biologist and the Director of the Medical Research Council( MRC) Laboratory of Molecular Biology( LMB) in Cambridge, UK. He became Director of the MRC- LMB in April 2018 taking over from Sir Hugh Pelham. Löwe is known for his contributions to the current understanding of bacterial cytoskeletons.
