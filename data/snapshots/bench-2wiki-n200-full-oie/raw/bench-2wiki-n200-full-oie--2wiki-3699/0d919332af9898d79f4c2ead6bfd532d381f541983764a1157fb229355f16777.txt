Hrana Vuković
Hrana Vuković (1370 – 15 March 1435) was a Bosnian magnate who ruled the area between Neretva and Drina rivers in Bosnia with the title "Grand Duke of Bosnia" prior to 1380. He was a father of Sandalj Hranić and brother of Vlatko Vuković. After his death, he was succeeded by Vlatko Vuković as a head of Vuković-Kosača family, and Hrvoje Vukčić as a Grand Duke of Bosnia.
