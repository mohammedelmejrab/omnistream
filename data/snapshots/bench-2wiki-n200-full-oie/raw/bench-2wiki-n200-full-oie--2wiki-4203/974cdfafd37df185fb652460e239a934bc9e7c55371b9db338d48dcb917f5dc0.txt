Fujiwara no Otomuro
Fujiwara no Otomuro( 藤原乙牟漏], 760 – April 28, 790) was a Japanese noblewoman and Empress consort of Japan. Her sister was Fujiwara no Moroane. Fujiwara no Otomuro was a daughter of a noble called Fujiwara no Yoshitsugu; her mother was the granddaughter of general Fujiwara no Umakai, who died in 737. She married Emperor Kanmu. Their children included: She also had a daughter, Princess Koshi. Her daughter- in- law was Lady Tachibana no Kachiko.
