Rudolf Steiner (film director)
Rudolf Steiner, b. 1942, is a German film director, scriptwriter and producer. Steiner studied at the Film& Television Academy( HFF)" Konrad Wolf" in Potsdam- Babelsberg. From 1971 to 1973 he worked as a cameraman for Sender Freies Berlin( SFB). As a producer he realized among other films" Conversation with the Beast"( director: Armin Mueller- Stahl).
