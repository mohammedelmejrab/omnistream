Teja Sajja
Teja Sajja( born 23 August 1995) popularly known as Master Teja is an Indian Telugu cinema former child actor turned hero who acted in popular films such as Chudalani undi, Yuvaraju, RajaKumarudu, Andarivadu, Ottesi Cheputunna, Leelamahal Center, Aakasha Veedhilo, Sree Ramadasu," Indra Tagore SambaBachi and Oh! baby."
