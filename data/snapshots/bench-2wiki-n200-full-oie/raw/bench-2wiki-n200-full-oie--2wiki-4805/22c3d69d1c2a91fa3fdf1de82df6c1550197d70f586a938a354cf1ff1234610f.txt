Otto III, Prince of Anhalt-Bernburg
Otto III, Prince of Anhalt- Bernburg( died 27 February 1404) was a German prince of the House of Ascania and ruler of the principality of Anhalt- Bernburg. He was the youngest son of Bernhard III, Prince of Anhalt- Bernburg, by his third wife Matilda, daughter of Magnus I, Duke of Brunswick- Göttingen.
