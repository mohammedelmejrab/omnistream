Gordon Chan
Gordon Chan Kar-Seung , born in 1960, is a Hong Kong film director, writer and producer. His most notable works include "Beast CopsFist of LegendPainted Skin2000 AD" and . In 2011 he announced and launched the productions for "MuralFour Detectives/Four Marshals", and "Painted Skin 2". Though for Painted Skin 2 he dropped out as the director and the director's seat was given to Wuershan. His 1996 film "First Option" was entered into the 20th Moscow International Film Festival.
