The Heart of Maryland (1921 film)
The Heart of Maryland is a lost 1921 American silent film feature produced and distributed by the Vitagraph Company of America. It is based on David Belasco's 1895 play," The Heart of Maryland". When Warner Brothers acquired the Vitagraph Studios in 1925, they obtained the screen rights to this property and remade the story in 1927 as" The Heart of Maryland" with Dolores Costello.
