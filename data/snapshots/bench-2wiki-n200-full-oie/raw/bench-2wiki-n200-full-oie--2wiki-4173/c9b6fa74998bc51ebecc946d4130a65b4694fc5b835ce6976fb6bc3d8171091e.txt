Vishukkani
Vishukkani is a 1977 Indian Malayalam film, directed by J. Sasikumar and produced by R. M. Sundaram. The film stars Prem Nazir, Sharada, Thikkurissi Sukumaran Nair and Sankaradi in the lead roles. The film has musical score by Salil Chowdhary. The film was a remake of the Tamil film" Karpagam".
