Nicole Briscoe
Nicole Briscoe (née Manske) is an American sportscaster who is employed by ESPN. Originally focused on covering auto racing for the network, which included stints as the host of "NASCAR Countdown" and "NASCAR Now", Briscoe became a "SportsCenter" anchor in 2015. She is married to IndyCar Series driver Ryan Briscoe, whom she wed in an outdoor ceremony in Hawaii in 2009.
