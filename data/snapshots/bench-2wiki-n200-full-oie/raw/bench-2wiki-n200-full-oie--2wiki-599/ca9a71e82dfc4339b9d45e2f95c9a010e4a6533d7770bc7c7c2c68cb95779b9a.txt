The Marriage of Princess Demidoff
The Marriage of Princess Demidoff( German: Die Ehe der Fürstin Demidoff) is a 1922 German silent drama film directed by Frederic Zelnik and starring Lya Mara, Charles Willy Kayser and Olga Limburg. It premiered at the Marmorhaus cinema in Berlin. The film's sets were designed by the art director Fritz Lederer.
