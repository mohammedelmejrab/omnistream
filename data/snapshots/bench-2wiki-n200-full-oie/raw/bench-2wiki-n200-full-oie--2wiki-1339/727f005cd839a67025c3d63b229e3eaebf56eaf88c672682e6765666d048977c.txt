Ravi Chopra
Ravi Chopra( 27 September 1946 – 12 November 2014) was an Indian film producer and director. He was the son of producer and director Baldev Raj Chopra and nephew of producer and director Yash Chopra. Aditya Chopra and Uday Chopra are his cousins. He is known for directing the epic Indian television series" MahabharatVishnu Puran" and" Aap Beeti".
