Sirivantha
Sirivantha is a 2006 Indian Kannada film starring Vishnuvardhan. This film, directed by S. Narayan, is the sixth and last film of the Vishnu- Narayan combination. Sirivantha followed the other 5 Vishnu- Narayan films" Veerappa NayakaSurya VamshaSimhadriya SimhaJamindaru" and" Varsha". Though the film was a remake of the Telugu film" Aa Naluguru", starring Rajendra Prasad in the lead, the Kannada version became more popular than the original and ran successfully for 100 days at the box office.
