Bernhard III, Prince of Anhalt-Bernburg
Bernhard III, Prince of Anhalt- Bernburg( died 20 August 1348) was a German prince of the House of Ascania and ruler of the principality of Anhalt- Bernburg. He was the eldest son of Bernhard II, Prince of Anhalt- Bernburg, by his wife Helene, daughter of Wizlaw II, Prince of Rügen.
