Hank Robinson
John Henry( Hank) Robinson( born John Henry Roberson; August 16, 1887- July 3, 1965) was a Major League Baseball pitcher who played from to and again in with three teams. He batted right and threw left- handed. He was born in Floyd, Arkansas and died in North Little Rock, Arkansas. He was inducted into the Arkansas Sports Hall of Fame in 1962. Roberson is also known as John Henry Robinson and Rube Robinson.
