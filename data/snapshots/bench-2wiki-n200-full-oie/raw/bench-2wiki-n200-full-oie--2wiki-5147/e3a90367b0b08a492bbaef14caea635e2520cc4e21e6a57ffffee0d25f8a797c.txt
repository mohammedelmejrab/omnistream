Hani al-Sayegh
Hani Abdel Rahim Hussein al- Sayegh is a Saudi citizen, and alleged member of Hezbollah Al- Hejaz and accused of involvement in the 1996 Khobar Towers bombing. He was one of 14 people subjected to extraordinary rendition by the CIA prior to the 2001 declaration of a War on Terror. Although he appeared to be innocent, American officials released a falsified account of his interrogations and deported him back to Saudi Arabia, where it was presumed he faced beheading. Despite the lack of evidence against him, Hani Al- Sayegh was one of the individuals named later in an indictment issued by the US Justice Department
