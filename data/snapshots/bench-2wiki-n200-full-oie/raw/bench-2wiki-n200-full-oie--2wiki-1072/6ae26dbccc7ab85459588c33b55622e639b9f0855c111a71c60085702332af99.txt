Blanche of Navarre, Countess of Champagne
Blanche of Navarre( ?–1229) was countess and then regent of Champagne and finally also regent of her native kingdom of Navarre. She was the youngest daughter of Sancho VI of Navarre and Sancha of Castile, who died in 1179, about two years after Blanche's birth. Her eldest brother, Sancho VII of Navarre, succeeded their father as King of Navarre and was the last male descendant of the first dynasty of Kings of Navarre, the Pamplona dynasty, dying childless. Her elder sister Berengaria of Navarre married Richard I of England.
