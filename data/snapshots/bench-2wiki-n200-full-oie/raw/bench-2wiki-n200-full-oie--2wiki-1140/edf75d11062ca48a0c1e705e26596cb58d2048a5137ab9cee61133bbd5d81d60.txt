Mariloup Wolfe
Mariloup Wolfe( born 3 January 1978 in Montreal, Quebec) is a Canadian actress and film director. She went to F.A.C.E. School, an art school in Montreal. She holds a major in Film Production from Concordia University( 2001) and a minor in Cultural Studies from McGill University( 1999). Mariloup Wolfe became famous through her role as" Marianne" in the popular TV series Ramdam broadcast since 2001 on Télé- Québec.
