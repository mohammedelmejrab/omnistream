OSS 117: Lost in Rio
OSS 117: Lost in Rio released in France as OSS 117: Rio ne répond plus( stylized as OSS 117: Rio ne répond plus ...), is a 2009 French spy comedy film directed and co-written by Michel Hazanavicius. It is a sequel to Hazanavicius', and see Jean Dujardin reprising his role as French secret agent Hubert Bonisseur de La Bath/ OSS 117. Set in 1967," Lost in Rio" sees OSS 117 sent to Brazil in order to retrieve a microfilm list of French Nazi sympathizers, only to once again unknowingly set foot into a bigger international intrigue. Like the first film," Lost in Rio" is based on the OSS 117 character from the novels by Jean Bruce, but acts as a parody rather than a faithful adaptation. The original title literally translates to" OSS 117: Rio does n't respond anymore", a reference to the 1932 film" F.P.1 Does n't Respond".
