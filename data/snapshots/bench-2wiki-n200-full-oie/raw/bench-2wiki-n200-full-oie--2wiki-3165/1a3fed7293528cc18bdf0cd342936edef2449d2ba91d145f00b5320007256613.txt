Marlon Riggs
Marlon Troy Riggs( February 3, 1957 – April 5, 1994) was an American filmmaker, educator( professor), poet, and gay rights activist. He produced, wrote, and directed several documentary films, including" Ethnic NotionsTongues UntiedColor Adjustment", and" Black is ... Black Ai n't." Riggs created aesthetically innovative and socially provocative films that examine past and present representations of race and sexuality in America. The Marlon Riggs Collection is now housed at Stanford University Libraries.
