Allen Winter
Allen Ernest Winter( 8 December 1903 – 8 July 1997) was the long serving second Anglican Bishop of the former Diocese of St Arnaud in north- west Victoria. Winter was educated at Melbourne Grammar School and University College, Oxford and ordained in 1928. He began his ordained ministry as a curate at Christ Church, South Yarra and then St James ’ Ivanhoe. After this he held incumbencies at Sunshine, Brighton and Essendon. From 1948 he was a canon residentiary at All Saints ’ Cathedral, Bathurst until his ordination to the episcopate.
