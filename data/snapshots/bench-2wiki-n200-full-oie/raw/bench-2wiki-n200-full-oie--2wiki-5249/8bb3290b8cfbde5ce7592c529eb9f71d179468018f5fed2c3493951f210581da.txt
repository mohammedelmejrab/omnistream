Never the Twain Shall Meet (1931 film)
Never the Twain Shall Meet is a 1931 drama film produced and distributed by Metro- Goldwyn- Mayer and starring Leslie Howard and Conchita Montenegro. It is based on the book by Peter B. Kyne. The film was directed by W. S. Van Dyke and was filmed in Tahiti like Van Dyke's two previous south sea adventures" The Pagan" and" White Shadows in the South Seas". The film is a remake of a 1925 silent film of the same name.
