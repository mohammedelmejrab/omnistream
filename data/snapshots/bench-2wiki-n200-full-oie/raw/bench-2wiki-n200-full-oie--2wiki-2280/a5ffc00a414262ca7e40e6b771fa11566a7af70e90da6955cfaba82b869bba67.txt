Stand by Me (Charles Albert Tindley song)
" Stand by Me" should not be confused with" Stand by Me Father", by Sam Cooke and James W. Alexander. Though sometimes known as" Stand by Me FatherStand by Me" is a 1905 gospel song by the Rev. Charles Albert Tindley. Although often thought to be a" traditional spiritual", Tindley copyrighted the song in 1905. The song of the same name by Ben E. King draws on Tindley's song. The song has been sung in Tindley's original gospel form by various artists including Bob Dylan.
