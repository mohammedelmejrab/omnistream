The Private Life of Louis XIV
The Private Life of Louis XIV or Liselotte of the Palatinate( German: Liselotte von der Pfalz) is a 1935 German historical film directed by Carl Froelich and starring Renate Müller, Eugen Klöpfer and Maria Krahn. The film's English language release title is a reference to the hit British film" The Private Life of Henry VIII"( 1933). The film portrays the life of the Heidelberg- born Elizabeth Charlotte, Princess Palatine, who married into the French royal family during the reign of Louis XIV. She was also the subject of a 1966 biopic in which she was played by Heidelinde Weis.
