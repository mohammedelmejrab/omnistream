Abdul Hamid Lahori
Abdul Hamid Lahori( died 1654) was a traveller and historian during the period of Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan who later became a court historian of Shah Jahan( Shah Jahan's official chronicler). He wrote the book" Badshahnama", about the reign of Shah Jahan. He has described Shah Jahan's life and activities during the first twenty years of his reign in this book in great detail Infirmities of old age prevented him from proceeding with the Third decade which was then chronicled by Waris, a historian.
