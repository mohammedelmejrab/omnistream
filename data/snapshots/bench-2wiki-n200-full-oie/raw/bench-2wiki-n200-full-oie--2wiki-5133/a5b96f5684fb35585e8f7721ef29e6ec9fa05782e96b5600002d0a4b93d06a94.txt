Stephen (song)
" Stephen" is a song by American recording artist and songwriter Kesha, taken from her debut studio album" Animal"( 2010). The song was written by Kesha in collaboration with David Gamson, Pebe Sebert, Oliver Leiber. It was produced by Gamson with additional production done by Leiber. The song's instrumentation was by Gamson, with the exception of the accordion, which was played by Kesha. Kesha wrote the song about a boy that she had been stalking and who had refused to call the singer. Musically," Stephen" is a dance- pop song that incorporates elements of country music. The song received some positive reviews from music critics upon its release. After the release of" Animal" for download," Stephen" charted on the lower regions of the South Korea Gaon International Chart. A music video for the song was released on the repackaging of the singer's debut album," Animal+ Cannibal".
