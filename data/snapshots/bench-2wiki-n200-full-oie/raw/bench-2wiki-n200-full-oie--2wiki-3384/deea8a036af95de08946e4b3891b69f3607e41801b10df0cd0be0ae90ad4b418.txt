Richard Astley (Warden of All Souls)
Richard Astley, D.D.( d 16 February 1636) was Warden of All Souls' College, Oxford from 1618 until his death. Astley graduated B.A. from Lincoln College, Oxford on 3 February 1593; M.A. from All Souls College, Oxford on 9 November 1596 and B.D. on 19 November 1606. He is buried in the chapel at All Souls, and his will was proved at Oxford 27 April, 1636.
