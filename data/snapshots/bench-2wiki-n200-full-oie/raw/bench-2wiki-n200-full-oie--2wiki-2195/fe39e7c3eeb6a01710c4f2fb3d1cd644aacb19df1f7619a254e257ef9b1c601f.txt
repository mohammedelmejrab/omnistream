Charlie Chaplin 2
Charlie Chaplin 2 is a 2019 Indian Tamil-language comedy film directed by Shakthi Chidambaram. The film stars Prabhu Deva, Nikki Galrani and Prabhu in the lead roles. The film is a sequel to 2002 film "Charlie Chaplin". The music for the film is scored by Amresh Ganesh. The film is produced by T. Siva under the production banner Amma Creations. The film released on 25 January 2019.
