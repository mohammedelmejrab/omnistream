The Courier of Lyon (1923 film)
The Courier of Lyon( French: L'affaire du courrier de Lyon) is a 1923 French historical drama film directed by Léon Poirier and starring Roger Karl, Daniel Mendaille and Émile Saint- Ober. It is based on the 1796 Courrier de Lyon case, which has been turned into many films including a 1937 French release" The Courier of Lyon".
