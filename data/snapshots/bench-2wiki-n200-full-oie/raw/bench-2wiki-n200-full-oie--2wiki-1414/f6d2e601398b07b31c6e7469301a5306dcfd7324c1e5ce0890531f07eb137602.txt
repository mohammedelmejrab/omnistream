The Love & War MasterPeace
The Love & War MasterPeace is the third studio album by American recording artist Raheem DeVaughn, released on March 2, 2010 through Jive Records. It was also released as a deluxe edition with an additional disc of bonus material. Recording sessions for the album took place during 2008 to 2009 and production was handled primarily by Kenny "Dope" Gonzalez. The album debuted at number nine on the U.S. "Billboard" 200 chart, selling 45,000 copies in its first week. Upon its release, "The Love & War MasterPeace" was well received by music critics.
