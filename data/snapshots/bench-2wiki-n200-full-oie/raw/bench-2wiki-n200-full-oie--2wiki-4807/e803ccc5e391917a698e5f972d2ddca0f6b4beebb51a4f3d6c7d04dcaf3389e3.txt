Bernhard II, Prince of Anhalt-Bernburg
Bernhard II, Prince of Anhalt- Bernburg( ca. 1260 – aft. 26 December 1323), was a German prince of the House of Ascania and ruler of the principality of Anhalt- Bernburg. He was the third son of Bernhard I, Prince of Anhalt- Bernburg, by his wife Princess Sophie, daughter of King Abel of Denmark.
