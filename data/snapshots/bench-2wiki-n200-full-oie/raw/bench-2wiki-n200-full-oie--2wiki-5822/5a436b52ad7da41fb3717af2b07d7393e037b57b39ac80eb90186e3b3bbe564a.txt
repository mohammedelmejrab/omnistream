Henry Wenceslaus, Duke of Oels-Bernstadt
Henry Wenceslaus, Duke of Oels- Bernstadt( also known as:" Henry Wenceslaus of Poděbrady Henry Wenceslaus of Bernstadt" or" Henry Wenceslaus of Münsterberg",,, or, or; 7 October 1592, probably in Oleśnica – 21 August 1639, probably in Bernstadt) was Duke of Bernstadt from 1617 until his death. He also used the titles of Duke of Münsterberg and Count of Glatz, although he never ruled those territories. From 1629 to 1639 he was Governor of Silesia.
