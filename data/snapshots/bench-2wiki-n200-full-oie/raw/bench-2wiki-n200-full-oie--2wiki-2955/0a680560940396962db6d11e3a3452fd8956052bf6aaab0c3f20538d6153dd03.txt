Manuel de Almeida (equestrian)
Manuel Rodrigues Tavares de Almeida Neto (born 11 December 1993) is a Brazilian dressage rider. He competed at the 2014 World Equestrian Games in Normandy where she finished 24th with the Brazilian team in the team competition and 87th in the individual dressage competition. His twin brother Pedro de Almeida and sister Luiza de Almeida have also been competing internationally for Brazil in dressage.
