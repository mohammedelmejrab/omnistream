The Pit and the Pendulum (1991 film)
The Pit and the Pendulum( released on DVD in the United States as The Inquisitor) is a 1991 American horror film directed by Stuart Gordon and based on the eponymous short story by Edgar Allan Poe. The film is an amalgamation of the aforementioned story with Poe's" The Cask of Amontillado", and it also appropriates the anecdote of" The Sword of Damocles", reassigning it to the character of Torquemada.
