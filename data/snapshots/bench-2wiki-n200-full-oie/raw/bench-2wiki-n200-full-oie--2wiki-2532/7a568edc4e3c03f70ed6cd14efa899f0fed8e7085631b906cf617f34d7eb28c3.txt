K-rupt
Carlton Williams Bongo Juma( 15 November 1979 – 29 November 2003), born in Nairobi and better known by his stage name K-Rupt, was a Kenyan rapper who had national success in the two years leading up to his murder in 2003. He collaborated with artists such as Fizzle Dogg, Bigpin, and E- Sir, who died earlier the same year. He also did concerts with fellow hip hop musician Nameless and was signed to the record label Ogopa Deejays. K-Rupt ’s hit singles in Kenya included “ Changamshwa, ” “ Tukawake, ” and “ Chacha. ” His single “ Dada Njoo ” was on the charts at the time of his death. The song “ Bamba ” with fellow rappers the late E- Sir and Bigpin became a hit. It had been recorded before his stint as a stand- by presenter at Nation FM.
