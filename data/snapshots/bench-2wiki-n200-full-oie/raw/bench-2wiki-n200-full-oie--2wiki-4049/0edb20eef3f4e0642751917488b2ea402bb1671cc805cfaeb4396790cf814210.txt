Angna Enters
Anita" Angna" Enters( April 18, 1907 – February 25, 1989) was an American dancer, mime, painter, writer, novelist and playwright. She studied at the Art Students League of New York and was a 1934 Guggenheim Fellow. She wrote a novel and three autobiographies as well as the films" Lost Angel"( 1943) and" Tenth Avenue Angel"( 1948).
