Carryn Sullivan
Carryn Elizabeth Sullivan( born 6 September 1955) is an Australian politician. Born at Millmerran, she achieved a Bachelor of Education and a diploma in primary school teaching. She was a Caboolture Shire Councillor from 1991 until she retired in 1994, and was secretary of the Bribie Island branch of the Labor Party. She held the seat of Pumicestone for Labor in Legislative Assembly of Queensland from its creation at the 2001 election until her defeat at the 2012 election. Her husband, Jon Sullivan, is a former MLA for Caboolture and former MP for the federal division of Longman.
