Emilio Botín (1903–1993)
Emilio Botín- Sanz de Sautuola López( 18 January 1903 – 22 September 1993) was a Spanish banker, the chairman of Santander Group from 1950 to 1986. He was born in 1903, the son of Emilio Botín López and María Sanz de Sautuola y Escalante. He was chairman of Santander from 1950 to 1986. He was married to Ana García de los Ríos y Caller. He was the father of Emilio Botín( 1934 – 2014).
