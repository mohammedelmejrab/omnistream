Torn from the Flag
Torn from the Flag is a 2007 documentary film about the international decline of communism and the 1956 Hungarian Revolution. The film encompasses the tense Cold War era( 1945 – 1991) and presents the rivalry of the superpowers during that time. It shows the 1956 Hungarian Revolution as the first catalyst for the future decline of the communist system, and as a remarkable turning point for the advancement of democracy. The film ’s Hungarian title is" A lyukas zászló". " Torn from the Flag" was made primarily for international theatrical release and television distribution.
