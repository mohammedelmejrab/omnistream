Adam Włodek
Adam Włodek (8 August 1922 in Kraków – 19 January 1986 in Kraków) was a Polish poet, editor and translator. He was married to Polish Noble Prize laureate, Wisława Szymborska between 1948 - 1954. He published 10 books, mostly collections of his poems. He was also the editor of several Polish newspapers including "Dziennik Polski", and translated works from Czech and Slovak languages into Polish.
