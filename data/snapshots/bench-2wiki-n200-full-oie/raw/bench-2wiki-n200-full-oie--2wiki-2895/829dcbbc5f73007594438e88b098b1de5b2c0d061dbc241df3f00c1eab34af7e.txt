Veerendra
Veerendra  was a Punjabi film actor, director, producer and writer who made 25 films in his 12-year career. Veerinder Singh, often known as Veerendra, made his debut with the film "Teri Meri Ek Jindri," released in 1975, featuring Dharmendra. He was a regular in Punjabi films of the 1980s. Some of his more popular films were "Lambhardarni, Balbiro Bhabhi" and "Dushmani Dee Agg," which was released after his death.
