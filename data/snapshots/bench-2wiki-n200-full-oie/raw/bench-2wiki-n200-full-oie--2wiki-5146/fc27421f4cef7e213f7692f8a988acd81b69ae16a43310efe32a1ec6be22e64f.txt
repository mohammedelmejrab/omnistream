Abdulatif Al Sayegh
Abdullatif Al Sayegh is a UAE businessman. As CEO of Arab Media Group Al Sayegh oversaw growth spearheaded by direction from the office of the Ruler of Dubai, Sheikh Mohammed Bin Rashid Al Maktoum. In 2010, he established Alsayegh Media, which he has grown into a 130 employee agency, spanning across two locations. Al Sayegh has benefitted from winning by and large, governmental contracts.
