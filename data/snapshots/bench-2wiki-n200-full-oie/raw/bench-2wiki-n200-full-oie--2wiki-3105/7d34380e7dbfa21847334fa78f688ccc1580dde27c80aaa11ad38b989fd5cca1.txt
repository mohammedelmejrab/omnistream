Robert Sackville-West, 7th Baron Sackville
Robert Bertrand Sackville- West, 7th Baron Sackville DL( born 10 July 1958) is a British publisher, author and guardian of Knole in Kent, which has been a Sackville house since 1603 and is now owned by the National Trust. The eldest son of Hugh Rosslyn Inigo Sackville- West and Bridget Eleanor Cunliffe, he inherited the title of Baron Sackville on 27 March 2004 on the death of his uncle, Lionel Bertrand Sackville- West, 6th Baron Sackville.
