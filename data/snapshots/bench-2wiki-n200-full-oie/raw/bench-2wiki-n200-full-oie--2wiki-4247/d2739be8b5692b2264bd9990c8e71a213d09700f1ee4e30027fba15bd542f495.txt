Michiel Vos
Michiel Vos( born December 19, 1970) is a Dutch- American journalist, lawyer, jurist and United States- based correspondent. He is married to filmmaker Alexandra Pelosi, daughter of Speaker of the United States House of Representatives Nancy Pelosi. They met in Amsterdam when Vos was working for the broadcasting station VPRO, as a presenter for special film nights. The couple has two children. Vos hosts a Dutch/ Belgian TV series called" My America" about his experiences as a European living in America. The HBO film Citizen USA was based on Vos's journey to become an American Citizen. Vos worked as producer on the HBO films:" The Trials of Ted Haggard", and. His wife Alexandra Pelosi is the sister- in- law of Peter Kaufman, who is the son of filmmaker Philip Kaufman.
