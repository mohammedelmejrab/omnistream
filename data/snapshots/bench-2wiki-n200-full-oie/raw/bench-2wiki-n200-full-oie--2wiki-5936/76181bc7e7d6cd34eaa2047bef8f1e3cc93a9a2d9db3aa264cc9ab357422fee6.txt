Christine Juliane of Baden-Durlach
Christine Juliane of Baden- Durlach( 12 September 1678 – 10 July 1707), was a German noblewoman member of the House of Zähringen and by marriage Duchess of Saxe- Eisenach. Born at Karlsburg Castle in Karlsruhe, Durlach, she was the oldest of the four children of Prince Charles Gustav of Baden- Durlach( younger brother of Frederick VII, Margrave of Baden- Durlach) and Anna Sophia of Brunswick- Wolfenbüttel.
