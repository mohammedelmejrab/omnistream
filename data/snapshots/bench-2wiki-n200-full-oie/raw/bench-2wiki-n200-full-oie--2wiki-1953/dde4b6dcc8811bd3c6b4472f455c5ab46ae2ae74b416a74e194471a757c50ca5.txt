Daniel Freire
Daniel Freire( born 29 December 1961, in Buenos Aires) is an Argentine film actor. He entered film in 1993 in" Un Muro de silencio" and has appeared in over 15 films including" Las Aventuras de Dios" 2000 in which he portrayed Jesus Christ and" Arizona Sur" in 2004. In 2005 he appeared in 27 episodes of the Spanish TV series" Motivos personales". He has lived in Spain since 1999.
