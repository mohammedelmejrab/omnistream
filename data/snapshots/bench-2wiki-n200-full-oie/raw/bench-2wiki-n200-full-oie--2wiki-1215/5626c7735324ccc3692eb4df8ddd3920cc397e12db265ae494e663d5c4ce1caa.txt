Daniel Barrett (visual effects supervisor)
Daniel Barrett is a visual effects supervisor. On January 24, 2012, he was nominated for an Academy Award for Best Visual Effects for the film" Rise of the Planet of the Apes". Barrett received his second Oscar nomination for the sequel" Dawn of the Planet of the Apes" at the 87th Academy Awards, and his third for" War for the Planet of the Apes" at the 90th Academy Awards.
