John IV, Count of Soissons
John IV( d. before May 1302), son of John III, Count of Soissons, and Marguerite of Montfort, daughter of Amaury VI, Count of Montfort. Count of Soissons, inherited upon his father ’s death in 1286. John married Marguerite of Rumigny, daughter of Hugh II of Rumigny and his wife Philippa. John and Marguerite had two children: Upon John ’s death in 1302, his son and namesake became count.
