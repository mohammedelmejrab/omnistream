William Louis, Prince of Anhalt-Köthen
William Louis of Anhalt- Köthen( 3 August 1638, in Köthen – 13 April 1665, in Köthen), was a German prince of the House of Ascania and ruler of the principality of Anhalt- Köthen. He was the second son of Louis I, Prince of Anhalt- Köthen, but second- born son of his second wife Sophie, daughter of Simon VI, Count of Lippe.
