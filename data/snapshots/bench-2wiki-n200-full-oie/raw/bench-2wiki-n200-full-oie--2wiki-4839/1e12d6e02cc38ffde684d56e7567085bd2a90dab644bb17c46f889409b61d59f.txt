Julio Nudler
Julio Nudler( 16 December 1941 – 27 July 2005) was an Argentine economic journalist. Nudler wrote for La Opinión, Clarín and La Razón before he became head of the economics section of Página/ 12 in 1990. In 2004 an article, in which he impeached Alberto Fernández who was the chief of the cabinet of Néstor Kirchner with corruption allegations, was not published by Página/ 12. Nudler condemned this as censorship and also accused his colleague Horacio Verbitsky of defending the government.
