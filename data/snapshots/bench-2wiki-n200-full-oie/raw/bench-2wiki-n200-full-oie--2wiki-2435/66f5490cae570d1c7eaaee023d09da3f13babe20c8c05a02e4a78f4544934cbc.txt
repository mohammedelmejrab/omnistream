Tony Klinger
Tony Klinger( born 29 January 1950) is a British film- maker, author and media executive. He began his career as Assistant Director on" The Avengers" in the 1960s, directed several rockumentaries and headed media companies both in the UK and the USA. Klinger is the son of film producer Michael Klinger, with whom he worked on the film" Get Carter"( 1971) starring Michael Caine.
