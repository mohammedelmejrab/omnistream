Fujiwara no Akisue
Akisue was close to Emperor Shirakawa, as his mother was the Emperor's nurse, and due to the influence of Fujiwara no Sanesue( 藤原 実 季), his foster father, who was Dainagon to the Emperor. Starting in 1075, Akisue held a number of local official posts, and by 1109 was appointed as a Dazai Daini( secretary to the administrative officer of several provinces). Akisue was the father of Fujiwara no Akisuke.
