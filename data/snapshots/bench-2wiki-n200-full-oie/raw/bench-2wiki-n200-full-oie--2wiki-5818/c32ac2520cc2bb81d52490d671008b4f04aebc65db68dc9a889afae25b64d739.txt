Henry XI of Legnica
Henry XI of Legnica( Schloss Liegnitz, 23 February 1539 – Krakow, 3 March 1588), was a thrice Duke of Legnica: 1551- 1556( under regency), 1559 – 1576 and 1580- 1581. He was the eldest son of Frederick III, Duke of Legnica, by his wife Katharina, daughter of Henry V, Duke of Mecklenburg- Schwerin.
