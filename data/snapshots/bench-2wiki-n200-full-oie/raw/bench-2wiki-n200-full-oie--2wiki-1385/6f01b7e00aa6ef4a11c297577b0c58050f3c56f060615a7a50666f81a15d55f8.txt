The Lawful Cheater
The Lawful Cheater, sometimes referred to as Lawful Cheaters, is a 1925 American silent crime- drama film written by Frank O'Connor and Adele Buffington The film was directed by O’Connor for B.P. Schulberg Productions, and starred Clara Bow, David Kirby, and Raymond McKee. After its 1925 U.S. theatrical release, the film was banned by the British Board of Film Censors. The film is presumed to be a lost film.
